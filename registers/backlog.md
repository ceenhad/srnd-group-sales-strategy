**How to read it.** Not everything is a project, and the Type column is doing real work: **Do** = work that
takes time; **Decide** = a choice only a named person can make, usually minutes; **Fetch** = a fact to look
up, no judgement required. **Status:** `ready` · `in progress` · `blocked` (named blocker) · `done` ·
`parked`. **The owner column carries roles, never names** — owner / authority / relationship / producer, defined in
`motion/motion-design.md` (S27); who fills each role is a separate decision. `—` means
unassigned, which is information rather than a gap to guess at. **When an item completes:** mark it done here, land evidence against the area document or the open item,
don't pass back through `group/`. **IDs are stable and rows regular** so the register can be lifted into
engine or any tracker when the process gets a system home. **A retired ID is not recycled** — gaps in a
sequence are expected, and `TSK-2` is one of them.

**This register files the work; it does not order it.** The workstreams `W0`–`W10` are a filing scheme.
**The order the critical-path items run in is in [`NEXT.md`](../NEXT.md)** — steps 0 to 5, with the dependencies

**Two more axes, added 2026-08-20 on Neil's observation that *"nothing seemed to separate a minor clarification
from a major point of strategy or internal development required — or even by brand and group."*** *The pattern is
`claims.md`'s `kind` column, which earned its keep by catching eight rows that were the constitution re-typed at
brand level.*

- **`Weight`** — **`strategy`** *(changes what the business does commercially: money, a commitment, what we sell,
  who to)* · **`capability`** *(internal development that must exist before anything can be said or sold — a build,
  a dataset, an instrument, a skill)* · **`clarification`** *(a wording, a fact, a filing choice — small, and not an
  owner's problem)*.
- **`Scope`** — **`group`** · a **brand** name · **`platform`** *(belongs to `cinema-platform`, not us)*. **The
  layer rule is a hard don't** (`../CLAUDE.md`), so a row whose scope is a brand must not be carrying a group
  decision, and the column is what makes that visible.

*A `clarification` row that is also typed `Ask` is a contradiction — it fails the bar and should be `Do`.*

**The two registers, and what separates them.** *A **task** needs a producer; a **question** needs Neil. So the
type says who acts: **`Do`** and **`Fetch`** are work somebody performs · **`Ask`** is a question that has not been
put to him yet, and is not workable until it has · **`Flag`** is something for another repo's owner.* **There is no
`Decide` type** — *a decision is not something a session performs. It is a question, asked in the box and answered,
and the answer lands in `questions.md` § Answered, which is the decision record* (`../CLAUDE.md`). **So a row typed
`Ask` is the queue of things to put to Neil; `questions.md` holds what has already been put to him.**
