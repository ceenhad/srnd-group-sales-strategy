# Channels (real, named)

`04-touchpoints.md` lists channel *types*; this is the actual named landscape to choose from —
the real places SRND can promote. Organised by kind, with what each reaches and the buyer-journey
stage it serves. Placement of content across them follows `10-content-placement.md`.

> **This is a candidate landscape, not a media plan.** Two disciplines apply (`CLAUDE.md`):
> 1. **Select, don't adopt wholesale.** Which of these SRND actually uses — and at what priority and
>    spend — is a decision (see `open-items.md`), not settled by listing them here.
> 2. **Verify before committing.** Don't assume membership or current usage, and confirm each
>    outlet's current name/status — trade media and events merge, rebrand and close, and this list is
>    written to a knowledge cutoff.

## Owned (no gatekeeper, always-on — the backbone)

These we control; everything else feeds into them (`09-brand-portfolio.md`, `06-content-production.md`).

- **The web properties** — `srnd.group`, the brand sites, `srnd.store`.
- **Email** — the trade list (registered partners and prospects).
- **YouTube channel + podcast** — the studio's output.
- **The Experience Centre** (Kirkbymoorside) — our own venue; and its virtualised form.
- **The virtual case-study / room-render library** — stories built on the renders.

## Trade press & media (residential CI / AV)

- **Essential Install (EI)** — UK CI trade title (already named in the Fabric Walls plan: advert →
  advertorial). Runs its own event (EI Live!).
- **HiddenWires** — UK custom-install title (online + print).
- **Inside CI / Connected** — UK/Australia CI trade press. *(Verify current title after industry
  consolidation.)*
- **CE Pro / CE Pro Europe**, **Residential Systems** — US/international CI trade.
- **Widescreen Review**, **Home Cinema Choice** — high-end home-cinema enthusiast/specifier titles
  (reach the end client and the passionate specifier; weigh trade vs consumer).

## Trade bodies, standards & awards

- **CEDIA** — the residential AV/home-cinema association (global, strong UK/EMEA). Membership,
  training/certification, the member directory, and the **CEDIA Awards** (credibility + reference —
  stage 5). Central to this industry; confirm SRND's membership/standing.
- **AVIXA** — the pro-AV body (commercial); relevant to the lever-2 commercial channel.
- **Industry awards generally** (EI, CE Pro, CEDIA) — recognition assets that feed proof and referral.

## Trade shows & events

- **ISE (Integrated Systems Europe)**, Barcelona — the largest European AV-integration show.
- **CEDIA Expo** (US) — the residential AV show.
- **InfoComm** (US, AVIXA) — pro AV (lever-2 commercial).
- **EI Live!** and UK regional events; **distributor open days**; **Futurebuild** and construction
  shows (lever-2 fit-out).
- **The Experience Centre** as our own standing "event" — the highest-trust venue we own.

## Social platforms

- **LinkedIn** (business + personal) — primary B2B channel; the trade lives here.
- **Instagram** — visual; rooms, installs, virtual case studies.
- **YouTube** — demos, how-to, and search (doubles as inbound).
- *(Weigh TikTok/consumer platforms case by case — mostly end-client, not trade.)*

## Communities & forums

- **AVForums** (UK) and **AVS Forum** (US) — large home-cinema communities. Mostly
  enthusiast/end-client with some trade; useful for demand and reference, handle with a trade-first
  posture.
- **CEDIA member community** and **LinkedIn groups** — trade-to-trade; peer referral lives here.

## Inbound / discovery

- **Google / SEO** and **AI answer engines (GEO/AEO)** — how a dealer finds us cold; the top of a
  direct-global funnel (`brands/c-ats/content-strategy.md` is the C-ATS instance).
- **YouTube search** — the studio's content working as discovery, not just presence.

## Lever-2 adjacent channels (higher uncertainty — validate per channel)

Per `brands/c-ats/growth-levers.md`; each new channel is a different buyer, so validate before spend.

- **Pro install / studios:** Sound On Sound, Resolution, Pro Sound News; AES, the Music Producers
  Guild.
- **Commercial cinema / DCI:** Cinema Technology Magazine (CTC), ISDCF, SMPTE.
- **Fit-out / interior design:** Dezeen and luxury-interiors press; BIID / SBID. More spec-influencer
  than dealer — a route to many rooms via the people who design them.

## How this connects

- **By stage:** owned + social + press + search do stages 1–2 (reach, proposition); press/awards +
  communities + the Experience Centre + virtual case studies do stage 3 (proof); events and peer
  referral run across; owned email + community carry stage 6.
- **By placement:** which *content* lands where across these channels is `10-content-placement.md`;
  which channels a given brand prioritises is that brand's `growth-levers.md`.
