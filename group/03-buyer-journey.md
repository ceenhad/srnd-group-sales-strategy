# The buyer journey

**This is defined at group level.** Each brand runs its own version; the stages, the difficult
repeat-order stage, and the cross-sell handoff are the same everywhere. What changes per brand is the
*content* presented at each stage (the proposition, the proof) — that lives in
`brands/<brand>/content.md`.

The journey runs from "never heard of us" round to "orders again" — a loop, not a one-way path. The
hard part, the part a human rep struggles with most, is the last stage: earning the *next* order.
Almost all of it is process; it just has to be done, and presented at the right moment.

1. **Unaware** → get in front of them.
   *Touchpoints: trade press, trade events, social, inbound search/AI, peer referral, the group's
   own existing dealers. Content: the hook — the brand's core problem, stated boldly.*
2. **Name lands, means nothing** → land the proposition: one system, a real problem they have, easy.
   *Touchpoints: social, email, website, a short video. Content: the range/possibility layer.*
3. **"Could this work for me?"** → prove it works, it's easy to spec/install, it won't embarrass
   them in front of their client.
   *Touchpoints: KB how-to content, virtual demo/webinar, sample kit, Experience Centre
   (`06-content-production.md`), a Zoom, the measured data. Content: the how-it-works layer.*
4. **Wants to buy** → register, then buy. Pricing is registered-partner-only by design
   (`07-portal-and-pricing.md`), so **partner registration *is* the stage-4 gate** — it's what turns
   an interested dealer into one who can see pricing and transact.
   *Touchpoints: the portal — partner registration, partner pricing, spec help, the design service.*
   **Shared gate:** registration serves every brand at once — build it once, in the portal.
   (See `07-portal-and-pricing.md` for current state.)
5. **First order, job on site** → make the first job succeed: install support, verification, make
   the dealer look good. → a satisfied dealer *and* a public-safe reference asset.
   *Touchpoints: install content, verification services, Zoom support.*
6. **The next order — the hardest stage.** Process, not hope:
   - **reorder ease** — saved details, one-click restock through the portal;
   - **new reasons** — a new element, or a new range when it ships (never before it ships);
   - **cross-sell into the group** — the next order may be for a *different brand entirely*;
     this is where "every dealer matters" (`01-dealer-as-asset.md`) pays off;
   - **persistent presence and low friction** — we can't manufacture the dealer's next project; it
     lands on *their client's* clock, not ours. So the approach isn't a push, it's being recalled
     the moment it lands and being effortless to act on. A satisfied dealer then refers a peer →
     back to stage 1.

> **The cross-brand handoff at stage 6 is group-owned.** A single brand's version of the journey
> can't own the step that hands to *another* brand — that coordination is why the buyer journey is
> defined at group level and not inside any one brand.
