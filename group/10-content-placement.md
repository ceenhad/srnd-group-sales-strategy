# Content placement

Where each kind of content lands across the group's properties, and the rules for choosing. This is
what turns the wide top of funnel (`02-commercial-model.md`) into something deliberate: many entry
points, but a clear home for each piece, so contact load is spread rather than concentrated.

It sits on top of three things already established: the web architecture (`09-brand-portfolio.md`),
the buyer journey (`03-buyer-journey.md`), and the content operation that produces the material
(`06-content-production.md`). Brands slot their own content into this map via
`brands/<brand>/content.md`.

## Placement rules

1. **Match the landing to readiness.** Broad, company-level material → the group site; specific,
   functional material → a brand site; purchase intent → the store. Ask the right amount at the
   right moment rather than over-asking.
2. **Lead simple; the complex story is earned.** Top of funnel leads with a single problem, simply
   put — one product, one brand, one clear hook. The whole-room, one-relationship story is the
   group's richest and most complex proposition; it is *never* the cold open. Hold it for engaged
   contacts, where it becomes the natural opener for cross-sell at stage 6.
3. **One primary home per piece.** Give each piece a single home and point to it from elsewhere;
   don't republish the same push across every property at once — that's how a shared contact gets
   overwhelmed.
4. **Group carries company-level and cross-brand; brand sites stay functional.** Anything about the
   whole room, the group, or more than one brand lives at group level. Brand sites stay lean —
   product, range, spec, proof.
5. **Route entry to spread load.** With many entry points, vary where a given contact comes in
   rather than funnelling everyone through one door. Placement is a load-management lever, not just
   reach.
6. **Off-site channels feed the properties, they don't replace them.** Social, press, events,
   search/AI, YouTube and email do the reaching; they hand off to a property that does the next job.

## The map

| Content | Primary home | Stage | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Simple intro + routing ("what we do", find your need) | Group site (landing pages) | 1 | Introduce simply, point to the right brand |
| Campaign / entry landing pages | Group site (or a brand site for a brand-specific push) | 1 | Capture a wide entry, spread load |
| The hook — the problem stated boldly | Off-site: social, trade press, events, search/AI | 1 | Get in front of them |
| Range / possibility (what's possible, data cards) | Brand site | 2 | Land the proposition |
| Virtual case studies (room renders — a large existing library) | Brand site (+ group site for whole-room) | 2–3 | Show the range in context; prove it in a real room type |
| How-it-works / KB / technical proof / measured data | Brand site (+ KB) | 3 | Prove it works, easy to spec/install |
| Recorded demos / virtualised Experience Centre | Brand site + YouTube; live via webinar/Zoom | 3 | Show the difference remotely |
| Sample kit / product in hand | Physical (post) | 3 | Tactile proof |
| Registration, partner pricing, purchase | Store (`srnd.store`; registration/pricing run by engine) | 4 | Register once, buy the whole room |
| Install / verification / support content | Brand site + store + Zoom | 5 | Make the first job succeed |
| Whole-room story / one relationship across the group | Group site (deeper) + email; shown once engaged | 6 | Open the cross-sell — how the room comes together |
| Reorder / new-element / cross-brand prompts | Store + email; group site for cross-brand | 6 | Earn the next order |
| Always-on presence (podcast, YouTube) | Off-site | 1 & 6 | Stay recalled; peer reach → referral |
| Evergreen trade collateral (brochure) | Downloadable, linked from all properties | 6 / ongoing | The thing a dealer (or the group) hands over |

## What this is not

It's not a publishing schedule or a channel calendar — those are execution, and (for the parts that
become live operations) engine's job. This is the standing answer to "where does this belong?", so
new content has an obvious home and the funnel stays wide without overwhelming anyone.
