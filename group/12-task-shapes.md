# Task shapes — a template and a definition of done for the `varies` tasks

*From the QA (`group/10-tasks-qa-sheet.md`), the "write the shape" move. Pass 1 found **19 tasks that vary by
whoever does them** — a habit, not a process — and pass 1's sweep B reduced the cause to two missing artefacts
per task: **`no template`** (nothing to copy from) and **`no standard`** (nobody decided what good looks like).
This file writes both. It is cheap, needs no engine and builds nothing — it surfaces the shape pass 1 said is
"already there and merely unwritten."*

**Three disciplines hold here.**

- **Surface, don't invent.** The template is drawn from what the repo already says the task involves —
  `group/11-work-items.md` decomposes each task into steps; `group/08-sales-motion.md` and
  `group/09-motion-design.md` design the motion. Where the repo is silent, a step is marked `[?]`, not filled.
- **A definition of done is often a decision, not a description.** Pass 1 named `no standard` as "nobody decided
  what good looks like," so where "done" is a genuine unmade call it is marked **`[decide]`** for the owner —
  once — rather than guessed. A proposed default is offered where the repo supports one.
- **Read the shape, not the cell.** The 19 are the aggregate finding, not 19 equally certain verdicts; several
  share a shape and will be grouped.

## `T-S03` — Qualify the dealer

**For:** deciding whether a dealer is worth pursuing, and on which brand — the same way each time, so it does not
depend on who picks up the enquiry.

**Template — each time:**
1. Pull what engine already holds on them — orders, brands bought, recency, registration (`retrieve`).
2. Write a one-line profile: what they buy, what they build, who they are (`assemble`).
3. Record a verdict — **pursue / hold / decline** — and on which brand (`judge`).

**Definition of done:** a recorded verdict against the dealer, with the one-line profile behind it, visible to
whoever picks them up next. **`[decide]`** the bar — what makes a dealer worth pursuing (build type? brand fit?
scale?). One decision, and the template applies it every time.

---

## `T-S05` — Present the range

**For:** showing a dealer the right part of the range for their business and their room — from the record, not
from memory, so every rep presents the same substance.

**Template — each time:**
1. Pull the on-ramp fields for the products in play — `D1`, `O1`–`O4`, `H2` — from the record (`retrieve`).
2. Assemble the material for *this* dealer and *this* room (`assemble`).
3. Have the conversation; read the room — the part no template carries (`relate`).

**Definition of done:** the dealer has seen the on-ramp for every relevant brand in their room, generated from
the record rather than recalled. **`[decide]`** the minimum set a "presentation" must always include, so nothing
is skipped because a rep forgot it. *(Depends on the record's on-ramp fields being filled — `DOC-13`, `KNW-*`.)*

## `T-M02` — Competitor tracking

**For:** keeping a standing picture of each competitor that accretes over time, instead of being rebuilt from
memory whenever it is needed. *(One of the duplication rows — the record side is `REC-13`.)*

**Template — each time a competitor is looked at:**
1. Note the change on their public surfaces — range, claims, pricing posture (`detect`).
2. Record what changed against what we held before, into `X1`–`X4` (`record`).
3. Judge whether it matters — a one-off, or a pattern (`judge`).

**Definition of done:** the competitor's record is current as of the look, and the previous state is still there
to compare against — so the picture accretes rather than restarts. **`[decide]`** which competitors are tracked
and how often. *(Where the record lands is the engine audit — `REC-13` / `REC-0`.)*
