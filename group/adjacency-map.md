# Adjacency map

*A prompt list for the specification conversation that is already happening — not an input to future
campaigns, not a strategy document. Source: `../NEXT.md`, lane 4 ("Cross-sell starts as a habit, not a
system"), smallest next move. Status: draft for use — amend from real conversations; evidence lands here,
it isn't re-argued in `group/`.*

For each entry point: the next layer(s) to raise, the physical adjacency that justifies it, and the moment
it's naturally relevant. Adjacencies come only from what the brand docs state; anything not stated is
marked **[?]**, not assumed.

## Entry: C-ATS (acoustic treatment)

- **Fabric Walls** — panels install behind the fabric wall (`c-ats/positioning.md` §1, §6). *Moment:* panel
  positions/quantities being decided — the fabric that covers them is the same conversation.
- **DT** — the Screen Wall carries C-ATS's acoustics through its structure and decoupling
  (`00-strategy.md`, "The whole room"). *Moment:* a front-of-room screen/masking build, not a plain wall.
- **Pro-Fi** — electronic calibration/voicing complements the physical treatment (`c-ats/positioning.md`
  §6; `pro-fi/positioning.md` §6). *Moment:* speaker system being specified alongside the treatment.

## Entry: Fabric Walls (surface)

- **C-ATS** — the fabric conceals the treatment behind it, same link reversed
  (`fabric-walls/positioning.md` §6). *Moment:* wall build-up being specified, before fabrication.
- **DT** — screens, port holes, LED and vents are set *into* the fabric surface
  (`fabric-walls/positioning.md` §6). *Moment:* the front-of-room element is being located within the wall.
- **Pro-Fi** — where fabric is acoustically transparent, speakers sit behind it
  (`fabric-walls/positioning.md` §1, §6). *Moment:* speaker positions being planned relative to the wall.
- **Light Walls [deferred]** — Fabric Walls Elements include LED/lighting details, so an adjacency exists,
  but Light Walls is a deferred brand: name it as an absence, not a pitch (`light-walls/brand-data.md`).

## Entry: DT (screens, masking, front-of-room structure)

- **Fabric Walls** — screen and masking integrate with the surrounding fabric
  (`display-technologies/positioning.md` §6). *Moment:* front-wall structure being designed.
- **C-ATS** — DT engineers structure and decoupling; C-ATS designs the acoustic requirement it must
  satisfy — align, don't duplicate (`display-technologies/positioning.md` §5, §6). *Moment:* any Screen
  Wall or structural front-of-room build.
- **Pro-Fi** — speakers sit within the front wall's baffle-mounted positions
  (`display-technologies/positioning.md` §6; `pro-fi/positioning.md` §6). *Moment:* an LED
  (non-transparent) screen — speaker placement becomes structural, not incidental.

## Entry: Pro-Fi (audio) — forward-looking

*Most of the range is pre-shipping (Pantheon nearing beta; Spatial's Dot 1 is what's actually shipping).
Raise it as capability, not a delivered system — say so plainly.*

- **DT** — with LED (not acoustically transparent), the Lattice places perceived sound at the image while
  speakers go where the room allows, in DT's Screen Wall positions (`pro-fi/positioning.md` §6).
  *Moment:* any project moving to LED.
- **C-ATS** — calibration/voicing sits alongside physical treatment; electronic vs. physical, not
  overlapping (`pro-fi/positioning.md` §6). *Moment:* treatment and speaker system specified in the same
  window.
- **Fabric Walls** — speakers are concealed by the fabric surface (`pro-fi/positioning.md` §6).
  *Moment:* speaker positions set, before the wall build-up is fixed.

## Entry: Light Walls (lighting) — deferred

A thin brand today — small range, little content, strategy deliberately deferred
(`light-walls/brand-data.md`). It pairs with Fabric Walls (LED/lighting details within the Elements range);
there is no settled adjacency story beyond that. Treat a Light Walls entry as an opening for Fabric Walls
only, and flag the rest **[?]** rather than invent a fuller map.

## Standing rules

- **Timing: fires on an established account, not at first order.** 74% of multi-line dealers bought their
  second line on a later date, median 246 days — eight months (`archive-findings.md` finding 4; `MON-4`).
  The natural moment is a live spec conversation on an account with one line already in, not first order.
- **Never steer the design tools toward our own products.** They stay brand-agnostic, serving whatever room
  is in front of the dealer — no specification-share weighting (`07-tools.md`).
- **After each spec conversation, capture three lines:** project; what the dealer was unsure about; what
  they were afraid of. Simultaneously the cross-sell trigger, the content backlog, and the buyer truth this
  repo marks **[?]** rather than guesses (`02-buyer-journey.md`; `NEXT.md` lane 4).
