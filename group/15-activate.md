# The activate plan — turning a latent moat into a working one

The moat (`14-moat.md`) is real but **latent**: the depth is unmatched, but it's untold,
under-proven, relationship-neglected, and concentrated in a few heads. A moat nobody sees defends
nothing. This is the plan to *activate* it.

## The promise we're making true and visible

**"You can trust we know what we're doing — and we've got your back."** Everything below serves
making that promise true (it largely already is) and *visible* (it isn't). Always dealer-first,
never a boast about our brilliance — mastery spent on the dealer, not on display (`14-moat.md`).

## Four workstreams

### 1. Tell — surface the untold depth, dealer-first
The coherence work: one true story flowing group → brand → product, in the trust/back voice, every
piece proving *understanding → help → the dealer's win* (`02-commercial-model.md`, content is the rep).
- **Deploy what's already built.** C-ATS has a full staged site + an 18-article KB sitting
  undeployed (blocked on a Shopify CLI re-auth). Shipping it is the cheapest, highest-leverage win
  we have. (`brands/c-ats/brand-data.md`.)
- **Feed the paid, empty channel.** The EI microsite is paid-for and near-empty
  (`group/12-channels.md`); fill it with company pages + virtual case studies.
- **Fix the neglected fronts.** Pro-Fi's social links are still Shopify placeholders; Fabric Walls /
  Light Walls are thin; only DT has YouTube. Connect the brand socials; close the YouTube gap.
- **Extend the content model.** C-ATS's GEO/AEO content strategy is the one plan-shaped asset from
  the ad-hoc period — extend it to the other brands.

### 2. Prove — evidence "makes you better" (the under-proven claim)
The "makes you better" payoff is asserted from capability, not yet demonstrated with dealers.
- **Case studies with measured before/after** — the strongest proof (and, per C-ATS's own strategy,
  the strongest AI-citation driver).
- **Virtual case studies** from the render library — *stories* built on the renders, not pictures
  (`group/06-content-production.md`).
- **Measured sign-off as the proof asset** — C-ATS verification, LWCP measured colour, Pro-Fi
  RP22/SMPTE verification, DT self-test. Proof the dealer can hand their client.
- **Named endorsements** from real dealers/pros (the Screen Excellence lesson, `group/13-competitors.md`).

### 3. Tend — build the appreciation that's currently at zero
The backing half of the promise, made tangible — the deficit named in `14-moat.md` and
`01-dealer-as-asset.md`.
- Real mechanisms that show dealers they're valued: recognition, reciprocity, loyalty,
  partner-not-wallet — most naturally at the repeat-order / ongoing-relationship stage
  (`03-buyer-journey.md` stage 6).
- Backing made real: install support, no truck rolls, being there after the sale.
- **Discipline:** don't *claim* appreciation until it's real — build it, then say it.

### 4. Institutionalise — out of heads, into systems
The moat is person-concentrated today. Turn the depth + dealer-understanding into reusable,
transferable assets (the product repos, design tools, measured data, the KB) so the advantage is the
group's, not one person's — and so activation scales past a small team.

## Sequencing (by leverage — this is a small team)

Quick wins first (cheap, near-done, high-leverage):
1. **Deploy the built C-ATS site + KB** (unblock the CLI). Near-done; ship it.
2. **Feed the paid EI microsite; fix Pro-Fi/social fronts.** Sunk cost, just needs using.
3. **Coherence messaging pass, brand by brand** — start with the sharpest instance (**DT**), which
   becomes the template for the rest.

Then, once there's something true and told to stand on:
4. **Proof** — case studies + verification assets per brand.
5. **Tend** — stand up the appreciation mechanisms at stage 6.
6. **Institutionalise** — capture depth/understanding as systems.

Last (per the existing sequencing in `open-items.md`): **paid spend**, once there's proof worth
promoting and validated audiences. Don't pay to amplify a story that isn't told or proven yet.

## What this connects to

This plan organises, under one frame, work already scattered across the strategy: the buyer journey
(`03`), content placement (`10`), the intro-campaign template (`11`), the channels (`12`), and the
open items (`open-items.md`). It's the concrete answer to the moat verdict's call to **tell, prove,
tend, institutionalise** — always pointing the depth at the dealer, never boasting it.
