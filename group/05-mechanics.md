# What "well" means — the mechanics

"Done well" falls straight out of the wheel (`03-the-wheel.md`), and it is as mechanical as it
sounds. It is mostly a checklist executed without holes and timed right — the skill is completeness
and timing, not brilliance. That's good news: it's enumerable.

- **No gaps.** Every stage has an asset and a touchpoint — nowhere the dealer stalls with nothing to
  move them on.
- **Every stage hands to the next.** A clear next step, end to end. This is the mechanical heart —
  the wheel only turns if each stage points at the one after it. No dead ends.
- **Right thing, right moment.** Triggered by the dealer's state changing (visited, ordered, went
  quiet), not by our sending schedule.
- **Friction killed at the buy** (stage 4) — the barriers resolved exactly where they bite.
- **Real machinery for the next turn** (stage 6) — not hoping they come back.
- **Clean cross-brand handoff** — the group must feel like one group at every stage-6 cross-sell, or
  the compounding leaks (`01-dealer-as-asset.md`).

This is "mechanically sound" at two scales. Each *piece* of content must hook, carry, prove, and
hand off on its own. The whole *journey* must do the same: cover every stage, wire every handoff,
time every trigger. Same discipline, one level up.

> **Group vs brand here.** The *checklist* is group-owned — it's the shared definition of "well" and
> the cross-brand handoff is only meaningful at group level. Filling it — the actual assets at each
> stage — is brand execution, done in each `brands/<brand>/`.
