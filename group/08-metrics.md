# Metrics

The unit is **the dealer relationship, across the group, over time** — not campaign reach and not
any single brand's revenue per order (`01-dealer-as-asset.md`). Judge by how many valuable dealers
are acquired and how deep they go across brands.

- **Discovery (inbound):** Share of Model — how often a brand is named in AI answers to buyer
  questions. Defined per brand in that brand's inbound/AI-discovery content plan (for C-ATS,
  `content-strategy.md`).
- **Buyer-journey progress (not yet instrumented):** dealers reaching each stage — partner
  registrations (the stage-4 gate, `07-portal-and-pricing.md`), first orders, *reorders and
  cross-brand orders* (the stage-6 signal that matters most), Experience-Centre/virtual-demo
  engagement. Targets set now would be guesses; set them once the partner pricing tiers and
  registration flow exist and are audited.

> **Why group-owned.** The metric that matters — cross-brand depth of a dealer relationship — can
> only be measured across brands. A per-brand dashboard can't see it, which is the whole point.
