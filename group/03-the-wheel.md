# The wheel — the buyer journey as a loop

**This is a group template.** Each brand runs its own instance; the stages, the "hard turn," and the
cross-sell handoff are the same everywhere. What changes per brand is the *content* presented at
each stage (the proposition, the proof) — that lives in `brands/<brand>/content.md`.

From "never heard of us" round to "orders again." It's a wheel, not a line: the hard part, the part
a human rep struggles with most, is the last turn — spinning it to generate the *next* order. Almost
all of this is mechanical; it just has to be done, and presented at the right moment.

1. **Unaware** → get in front of them.
   *Touchpoints: trade press, trade events, social, inbound search/AI, peer referral, the group's
   own existing dealers. Content: the hook — the brand's core problem, stated boldly.*
2. **Name lands, means nothing** → land the proposition: one system, a real problem they have, easy.
   *Touchpoints: social, email, website, a short video. Content: the range/possibility layer.*
3. **"Could this work for me?"** → prove it works, it's easy to spec/install, it won't embarrass
   them in front of their client.
   *Touchpoints: KB how-to content, virtual demo/webinar, sample kit, Experience Centre
   (`06-production-engine.md`), a Zoom, the measured data. Content: the how-it-works layer.*
4. **Wants to buy** → register, then buy. Pricing is registered-partner-only by design
   (`07-portal-and-pricing.md`), so **partner registration *is* the stage-4 gate** — it's what turns
   an interested dealer into one who can see pricing and transact.
   *Touchpoints: the portal — partner registration, partner pricing, spec help, the design service.*
   **Shared gate:** registration is a group mechanism serving every brand at once — build it once,
   in the portal. (See `07-portal-and-pricing.md` for current state.)
5. **First order, job on site** → make the first job succeed: install support, verification, make
   the dealer look good. → a happy dealer *and* a public-safe reference asset.
   *Touchpoints: install content, verification services, Zoom support.*
6. **The next order — the hard turn.** Machinery, not hope:
   - **reorder ease** — saved details, one-click restock through the portal;
   - **new reasons** — a new element, or a new range when it ships (never before it ships);
   - **cross-sell into the group** — the next turn of the wheel may be a *different brand entirely*;
     this is where "every dealer precious" (`01-dealer-as-asset.md`) cashes out;
   - **persistent presence + zero friction** — we can't manufacture the dealer's next project; it
     lands on *their client's* clock, not ours. So the mechanism isn't a push, it's being recalled
     the moment it lands and being frictionless to act on. A happy dealer then refers a peer →
     back to stage 1.

> **The cross-brand handoff at stage 6 is group-owned.** A single brand's instance of the wheel
> can't own the turn that hands to *another* brand — that coordination is why the wheel template
> lives at group level and not inside any one brand.
