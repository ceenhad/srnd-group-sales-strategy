# Touchpoints

There aren't many, and that's the point — they're enumerable. This document is the channel *types*;
the actual named channels (EI, CEDIA, ISE, LinkedIn, AVForums, and the rest) are in
`12-channels.md`. The outbound set the group already thinks in: **social, email, trade press, trade
events.** The rest that genuinely add:

- **The portal itself** — not a channel, the hub; everything routes here and it's a touchpoint every
  landing. Shared across all brands (`07-portal-and-pricing.md`).
- **Inbound search / AI answers** — how a dealer finds us when *they* go looking. In a direct-global
  model this is the top of the funnel. Each brand runs its own inbound/AI-discovery content plan
  (for C-ATS this is the GEO/AEO `content-strategy.md`); the group frame is that this sits at the
  top of every brand's buyer journey.
- **Owned video / YouTube + podcast** — a touchpoint class of its own, and what the studio
  (`06-content-production.md`) produces. Shared asset, per-brand output.
- **Live virtual demo / webinar / online CE training** — Zoom doing the rep's job and the
  credibility job at once.
- **Sample kit / product in hand** — tactile; a physical sample sells a hidden or hard-to-photograph
  product better than any image.
- **Peer referral** — dealers trust dealers; engineered off reference jobs (stage 5).
- **The group's own existing dealers** — a dealer of one brand is a warm audience for another; the
  cross-sell route runs both ways. This touchpoint is group-native — no single brand owns it.

Most are content-fed: the touchpoints are just where content is presented. Which is why the
content operation (`06-content-production.md`) matters as much as the channel list.
