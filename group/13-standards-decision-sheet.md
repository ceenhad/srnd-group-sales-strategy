# The standards to set — a decision sheet

*The `[decide]` calls from `group/12-task-shapes.md`, collected into a run sheet. Pass 1 found the 19 `varies`
tasks fail on `no standard` — **nobody decided what good looks like** — so these are the decisions that turn the
templates into standards. Each is minutes. Once set, the answer replaces the `[decide]` in the matching shape,
and the task stops varying by whoever does it.*

## How to run it

Same method as the QA: **one at a time, accept the proposed default or override it.** A proposed default is a
**starting point, not a fact** — it is there so the fast answer is "yes, that" and only the ones you'd change
need thought. Where a decision needs your real business truth rather than a sensible guess, it is marked **⚑**
and the proposal is explicitly a draft to correct.

*When answered, each result lands back in `group/12-task-shapes.md`, replacing the `[decide]` in that task's
shape. Nothing here is settled until you say so.*

> **Run 2026-08-14 — a first pass, closed as a pass, not as a settlement.** All 16 taken one at a time with
> Neil. **9** defaults accepted or amended outright (4, 7, 9, 11, 13, 14, 15, plus the amended 10 and 16),
> **3 reframed** (1, 2, 3 — the bar wasn't the question the default assumed), **1 struck** as unmeasurable (6),
> **2 left as build items** rather than settled artefacts (3, 8), and **1 still pending** a name from Neil
> (5, the roster). The original proposed defaults below are left in place — the outcome column is what landed.
>
> **Neil's caveat on the whole run, verbatim in spirit: these are good hints, not deeply qualified answers —
> "much of that needs each a session."** So the outcomes are *directions*, not decisions: the straight
> default-accepts can operate as working standards until their session contradicts them, but nothing here
> enters `decided.md`, and the substantial ones are session-sized: **the dealer-bar economics · the
> pre-sales/consulting boundary · the upsell proposal design · the daily-per-brand production question · the
> competitor roster · the per-brand `G2` lists.**

## Reading the result

Three of the sixteen carry the **⚑** — the dealer bar, the competitor roster, and each brand's `G2` — because
they need real business or brand truth and a guess would be worse than a gap. The other thirteen have safe
defaults; expect most to be a "yes, that." **The ⚑ three are the ones worth actual thought**, and the dealer bar
(1) is the highest-leverage of the lot — it sets the top of the whole funnel, and every qualification downstream
inherits it.

*When each is settled, it replaces the `[decide]` in `group/12-task-shapes.md`, and that task is now a standard,
not a habit.*
