# The group play — in one page

SRND is a **house of brands over one shared store.** Awareness is built per-brand; buying happens
in one place (the portal). The whole commercial machine exists to acquire and compound one thing:
**precious dealer relationships**, counted across every brand, over years.

**The spine is the wheel** (`03-the-wheel.md`): the buyer journey from "never heard of us" round to
"orders again" — and, critically, cross-sells into another brand. The wheel is a *group template*;
each brand runs its own instance of it, over a largely shared dealer base.

**Why the group frame changes the economics.** A dealer won for one brand is a natural buyer for
the others, because the brands serve the same room — most of the time a cinema/AV project that pulls
several brands in. So a small order from any one brand is not a low-value account; it's the opening
of a group relationship that compounds. This is why there is no "long tail to service cheaply," and
why direct-to-dealer (not distributor-led) is asset-building, not cost-saving.

**Two things make "done well" affordable for a small team:** the shared **production engine**
(studio + Experience Centre, `06-production-engine.md`) that makes rep-quality content cheap at
volume, and the shared **portal** that carries registration, gated pricing and reorder friction-kill
in one place for every brand.
