# Display Technologies — brand data (captured)

Raw input for the messaging work, not settled positioning. **Source:** the DT website
(`displaytechnologies.co.uk`) and the group site, captured 2026-07-23. Quoted/most phrasing is the
site's current wording, not ours.

## What it is (per the site)

- Site: **`displaytechnologies.co.uk`** (Shopify store; trades socially as "DT" / "DTScreens").
- Self-description: *"A British brand specialising in advanced projector screens, mounting brackets
  and custom installation solutions. Our focus on the combination of technical performance and
  design for install ensure that all DT products meet the needs of both the client and installer."*
- Homepage banner: *"World Leading Projection Screens."*
- Positioning cues on site: screens and mounts *"in some of the most prestigious home theatres
  globally"*; mounting solutions *"offer designers the ability to locate projectors in spaces that
  otherwise would not be possible"*; used for *"World Record breaking events"* and theatre — *"enable
  designers to think outside the box and deliver installations that would not normally be possible."*

## Range (real, from the site)

- **Projection screens:** Fixed Frame (modern styling, ease of installation, available to exact
  size); **Masking** (*"Mask Position Logic"*-based control); **Artmask** (high-resolution
  retractable artwork that protects/hides the viewing surface); Projection Image Surfaces.
- **Mounts & mirrors:** Vertical Projector Mirror Mounts (vertical mounting, several orientations);
  Side Projection Mirrors (first-surface optical mirror to redirect the light path); Ceiling
  Projector Mounts (2-part rapid-install framework, large projectors); Motorised Projection Mirror
  Drops (in-ceiling, dual high-performance optical mirrors).
- **Airflow:** Inline Fans, Fan Controllers.
- **Enclosures:** Projector Hush Boxes (isolate a projector in a housing); Port Holes (project from
  an adjacent room).
- **Spares.**

## Distinctive elements (candidate differentiators — to weigh in messaging)

- **"Design for install"** — the recurring theme: modular builds, ease of installation, made to
  exact size, rapid-install frameworks; meets the needs of *both client and installer*.
- **Mask Position Logic** — DT's named approach to masking screens / the aspect-ratio problem (also
  the subject of a DT article on the EI microsite).
- **Mounting that enables the otherwise-impossible** — placing projectors where they otherwise
  couldn't go; the "think outside the box" angle.
- **DT Create** — a tool to *"create configurable product drawings and specifications"* plus
  purpose-made design tools. A spec/config aid for installers.
- **Enthusiast Range** — a distinct range (`/collections/enthusiast`); role/audience to clarify
  (enthusiast/prosumer vs pure trade).
- **Learning & Support**, **Brand Assets** page, **Distributor Locator** — trade-support furniture.
