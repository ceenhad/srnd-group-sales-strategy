# Display Technologies — product line (catalogued from the website)

Source: `displaytechnologies.co.uk/products.json` (live Shopify catalogue), captured 2026-07-26.
52 live products. This is a factual catalogue for the strategy work, not positioning. Prices omitted
(trade-gated / placeholder). Two store vendors appear: *Display Technologies* and *SRND Store*.

The line resolves into six functional groups: **projection screens**, **image surfaces**, **projector
mounts & concealment**, **port holes**, and the **control + airflow layer** that drives the moving
products.

## Strategic reads (for the positioning work)

- **The Commanders are the control layer, and the differentiator.** DT doesn't just sell screens and
  mounts — it sells the system that *moves and manages* them: Actuator Commander 2 drives the dynamic
  masking and mirror drops; Mistral Commander manages projector airflow. This is the "control" leg of
  the group story, and where DT's real depth sits.
- **Products are matched to specific projectors.** Almost every mount, port hole, fan and Hush Box is
  tagged to exact projector models (Barco Residential, Christie, BenQ, Epson, DPI, NEC…). DT is an
  integration system built around the installer's actual kit — "we already know your projector" — not
  a generic accessory range.
- **A clear tiering exists in screens:** premium motorised **Dynamic** range vs entry **Enthusiast**
  (Mask / Outline). Worth settling how that tiering is named and sold.
- **Acoustic-transparent surfaces are a direct C-ATS / whole-room link** — the screen passes speaker
  sound; the treatment and speakers sit behind. Cross-brand coherence point.
- **Concealment is a theme across the line** — mirror drops, hush boxes, port holes, vertical mounts
  all exist to hide or quiet the projector. Pairs with the Fabric Walls "aesthetic layer" thesis.
