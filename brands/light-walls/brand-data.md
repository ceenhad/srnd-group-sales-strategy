# Light Walls — brand data (captured)

Raw input, not settled positioning. **Source:** the Light Walls website (`lightwalls.co.uk`) and the
group site, captured 2026-07-24. **This brand is thin right now** — little site copy, a small range,
light social — so there isn't much to capture yet; documented so we can return.

## What it is (per the site)

- Site: **`lightwalls.co.uk`** (Shopify). Headline: **"Advanced RGBW Light Engines."**
- Pitch: *"Open up a new dimension of dynamic lighting systems with quality that exceeds traditional
  solutions… the flexibility of addressable lighting… 16-bit dimming, incredibly high CRI, and wide
  saturation. Powerful integration tools and expert project support."*
- Buyer: *"innovative lighting professionals who demand addressable lighting that dramatically
  extends their design and deployment opportunities without compromising performance or reliability."*
- Model: browse light engines + system kits online; **supply-only or complete solution.**

## Range (from the site)

- **Down Light** — Down Light Module (RGBW); Down Light System Kit (RGBW, 20W, 70mm).
- **Linear** — Linear 1×10 (RGBW, 16-bit).
- **Matrix** — Matrix 5×5, 1×5, 1×1 (RGBW, 16-bit).
- **System Kits.**
- Content sections: Thoughts, Technical, Gallery, Profile (all light).

## Distinctive elements (candidate differentiators — to weigh)

- **Individually-addressable RGBW** with **16-bit dimming**, high CRI, wide saturation — quality/
  control beyond traditional installation lighting.
- **Integration tools + project support**; supply-only or complete.
- (Small, focused range — down light / linear / matrix modules + kits.)

## Cross-brand link

Light Walls is the **lighting layer of the whole-room build** — it pairs with Fabric Walls (whose
Elements include "LED Divider" and "Halo LED" details) and with cinema/media rooms generally. In the
group's whole-room play it's the addressable-lighting component behind/within the fabric surface.
