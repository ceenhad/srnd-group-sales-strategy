# Pro-Fi — brand data (captured)

Raw input, not settled positioning. **Source:** the Pro-Fi website (`pro-fi.uk`) and group site,
captured 2026-07-24. **Read `opportunity.md` alongside** — the website is thin/out of date and badly
undersells the brand; the platform is where the substance is.

## What it is (per the site)

- Site: **`pro-fi.uk`** (Shopify). Title: *"Pro-Fi Speakers & Amps | Professional Fidelity, Luxury
  Design."* Contact: `team@pro-fi.uk`, +44 (0)1751 646911 (North Yorkshire).
- Positioning lines: *"Experience unparalleled audio elegance," "Discreet pro fidelity audio,"
  "Imagined for immersive environments," "Professional fidelity, luxury design."*
- Pitch: *"a meticulously engineered range of speakers, subwoofers and Dante-enabled DSP amplifiers
  built for high-channel-count spatial audio — with understated luxury in every detail."*

## Range (per the site)

- **Full-range speakers** — compact, DSP-optimised, *"exceptional performance from an impossibly
  small solid aluminium enclosure."*
- **Subwoofers** — sealed, *"dual ceramic drivers… the output of a much larger system in an
  impressively compact chassis."*
- **Amplifiers** — Dante-enabled DSP amps; *"the right power, processing and connectivity for every
  install."*
- (The full engineered range — Spatial / Cinema / Modular / Stage / LFE — is in `opportunity.md`.)

## Distinctive elements (candidate differentiators — to weigh)

- **Professional fidelity + understated/discreet luxury** design; solid-aluminium enclosures.
- **High-channel-count spatial/immersive** focus; **Dante/AoIP-native**, DSP-optimised, active.
- Compact form factors punching above their size (small mains; compact sealed subs).
