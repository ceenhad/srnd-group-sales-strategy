## What this file is not

- **Not copy.** These are angles with substance behind them. **The wording is an owner's to set** — a hook is the
  one place the brand speaks to a stranger, and it goes out in a person's words, not a draft's.
- **Not a casting plan.** Multiplying hooks is cheap and comes *after* the archive count says what to multiply
  (CON-3; `content-plan.md` tranche 5). What is here is the honest candidate set, not a schedule.
- **Not a place for figures.** Data belongs in the depth layers a hook points at. A hook carries an idea; the
  number is the reward for following it.
- **Not a ranking of the panels.** The doors are grouped below because a hook has to name one problem to be
  specific. **C-ATS is a system and the 3 Rs is what defines it** — no panel leads (Neil, 2026-08-18,
  `../../registers/questions.md` Q50), and the range-level angles at the foot are the ones that say so.

## The three tests every candidate had to pass

1. **The stranger test** (S23). *Would someone who hasn't bought and doesn't know us stop for this?* Detail that
   presumes ownership or imminent install — fixings, site conditions, fitting tips — fails, and lands at stage 3
   or stage 5 instead.
2. **Evidenced and positive** (S23). A search hook is a claim that people actually type those words, and that
   claim comes from real questions, never from imagining a searcher. Early-journey pieces lead with the win, not
   the failure mode.
3. **The professional bar.** A hook has to teach a good integrator something they would respect us for knowing.
   Basics, convenience facts and raw data are not hooks — they are what the depth layer is for. **This test did
   most of the rejecting below**, and it is the one that keeps the estate from filling with filler.

## The blocked hook — corrected 2026-08-18

**An earlier draft of this file said the self-serve route "is not true yet" and that the quantity logic was
trapped in the legacy CATS Calculator. That was wrong.** Neil: *"how many panels — please see Cinema Tools Pro."*
The method is systemised, encoded and running — `cinema-platform` `products/cinema-tools/`,
`docs/product/c-ats-system-design-rules.md` and `engines/modal_analysis/`, with a worked reference (Joppa Road,
Gold on carpet = 11 boxes). See `pathway-resonance.md`.

**So the hook is blocked on the surface, not on the substance** — and the surface is further off than a second
draft of this file claimed. **ADR 017 v2 (accepted 2026-08-13) withdrew Level 2 entirely and made Cinema Tools
Pro internal tooling with no external login.** So there is no self-serve sizing route for a dealer today, and the
one that was planned was cancelled rather than deferred. Whether C-ATS gets one — a free Level 1 calculator,
published worked examples, or neither — is **DR-Q52**. **Build it, then say it** (`../../CLAUDE.md`): this hook
waits on that decision, not on a piece of writing.

hooks are the ones that do not sell a panel.**

## Placement, against the settled roster

Per Q49 (Neil, 2026-08-17; `../../group-strategy/strategy.md`):

- **Social carries the hooks, three-up** — LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram get the same piece, scheduled. Social
  carries the hooks and not the substance, which is exactly what this file is.
- **The brand knowledge base is where every hook hands to**, weekly. **A hook is not approved without its
  destination**, and today's two live hooks have none — CON-8 is therefore the first job in this file, ahead of
  casting anything new.
- **YouTube weekly** carries the explainer form, where the estate's only proven hook already lives.
- **Trade press and the EI microsite** carry the longer arguments — the depth, sequencing and why-to-spec angles.

**Search placement is provisional throughout.** Every "the problem named" row is a claim that dealers type those
words, and that claim is CON-3's to confirm. Until it does, they are cast on social — where the wording costs
nothing to change — and not built as search articles.

## What this set says about the brand

- **Twenty-five angles: two live, eight blocked, fifteen castable — and both live ones end dead.** The hook layer
  is not short of substance; it is short of connections, of an owner's wording, and of a decision about what may
  be said.
- **The best angles are the ones nobody has said, and the best of those were not in the brand folder.** Project
  sequencing (`N6`), the scatter-versus-absorb distinction and "check the test configuration" sit in the product
  record; the six strongest sit in the platform's design rules, where a brand run would not have looked until
  Neil pointed at them. **None of them needed inventing** — which is the production line's claim, tested, and a
  reminder that "the brand's material" is wider than `brands/c-ats/`.
- **The professional bar rejected more than the stranger test did.** Most of what fell out was true and useful
  and simply not something an integrator would respect us for saying. That is the filter worth keeping.

### What the mapping shows

- **`M2` is the moment a dealer cannot get past, and it is the worst served.** *They cannot quote without a number;
  the hook that would answer it is blocked on `Q59`, and the price is open on `Q45`. **Everything else in this file
  is upstream of a gate we have not opened.***
- **One hook reaches the specifier cast** — the timing argument, `N6`. *Against `C2.27` and a competitor partner
  running three ISE sessions at architects and interior designers, that is the gap, and it is the same gap
  `training.md` found: four of five rows spoke to the integrator.*
- **The two families that survived today's corrections are the two that talk about the dealer's job** — *"easier to
  do"* and *"more revenue"*. **Both demoted hooks were about our evidence.** *`PR-4` in one line: the appeals that
  survive contact are the ones where the dealer is the subject of the sentence.*
