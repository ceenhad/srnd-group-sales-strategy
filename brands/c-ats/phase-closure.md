## Where it stands after 2026-08-21

**Sixteen questions answered in one day, and the shape of the phase changed rather than the volume of it.** *`Q82`–`Q96`,
all recorded verbatim in `../../registers/questions.md`. Seven C-ATS `Ask` rows closed; five remain.*

**The pattern in what closed is the finding.** *Almost nothing was blocked on research. Each block turned out to be a
decision nobody had put to an owner, or a belief about the record that reading a primary source dissolved:*

- **`T1`** was carried as blocked on one missing EN 13501-1 number. *The fire position is **five positions across four
  materials**, and the piece's most useful section — the exposed lining surface is the fabric, not our panel — was in
  nobody's notes. **Drafted, in two tiers** (`Q93`).*
- **`DOC-8`** closed with a negative: the BSRIA report covers no marine panel. *That produced `DOC-32`, which closed
  the same day (`Q83`), which produced `DOC-41`, which closed the same day (`Q84`). **Three rows, one afternoon, from
  reading nineteen pages.***
- **`DAT-1`** was a data fix, then a strategy question, then a data fix again. *Settled by `Q82`: the panel is and always
  has been 43 mm and BSRIA logged a nominal.*
- **`KNW-4`** closed by being reduced twice, then answered on cost with a measurable revisit trigger (`Q86`, `ENG-27`).
- **`DR-Q52`** blocked training rows for weeks. *`Q90` supplied the test and `gating-sort.md` applied it — and the
  strongest material turned out to be on the public side.*
- **`Q45`** closed after four days: shape (`Q89`) then number (`Q94`).

**All five training rows are now runnable**, and the leg waits on an owner's calendar rather than on information
(`training.md`).

## The honest assessment

**The phase is not circling — but it reads as circling, and the reason is worth naming.** *Every answer today opened
one or two smaller questions, because each one reached a level of specificity the record had never had. **That is a
ratchet, not a loop**: `Q82` closed a premise, `Q83` closed a document, `Q90` closed a category of decision. Nothing
answered has been reopened.*

**What would make it feel finished is a different act from answering more questions.** *The five open questions are all
product specification — they belong to whoever designed the panels, not to a marketing phase. **The phase's own
deliverables are complete** (`../_template/the-brand-run.md`, five of five), the drafts exist, the corrections are
written, and the training leg is runnable. *What is left is applying the written copy at source and getting the pieces
delivered to a room.*
