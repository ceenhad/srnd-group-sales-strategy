# C-ATS — product & pricing (the range behind the group gate)

The group pricing **policy** (fully gated, no public or end-user prices) is set in
`../../group/01-commercial-model.md`. This file holds only C-ATS's range and the brand-level
tier work that sits behind the shared gate.

## Range

- **Treatment (the 3 Rs):** REF-CP, RES-CP, REV-CP-50.
- **Services / systems:** Acoustic Treatment Design Service (single service — the old Basic/Full
  split is gone), Acoustic Verification, Isolation System, Isolation Verification.
- **REF-CP-MAR** (marine) — a narrow compliance edge case for superyacht installs only. Not a
  segment; don't give it segment-level prominence.
- **REV-CP-12 is discontinued** — do not list it.

Isolation and Verification sit **outside** the 3 Rs (isolation decouples the room from the building;
verification is proof, not treatment). They live under C-ATS by category logic — C-ATS is where
acoustics lives, and acoustics includes both treatment and isolation — not by thesis expansion.

## Pricing tiers (behind the gate — numbers still to set)

Publication is settled (fully gated). What is *not* set is the tier structure itself:
- partner pricing tiers for the range;
- MOQs, design-tool access, CE credits, co-marketing terms as they apply to C-ATS.

These feed the shared portal's gated pricing; setting the numbers is a decision, flagged in
`open-items.md`.
