## The entry, re-keyed

**`PR-14` broke the framing all three doors opened on** — a room that already sounds wrong. Neil: *"the assumption
that they are asking because a room exists to be cured would be totally wrong. It's all built in from the initial
design stage."* **The specification happens before the room exists.**

**So entry is by project moment, not by symptom** (`claims.md` `C2.24`–`C2.26`, Neil: *"in fact all 3 should be
valid"*):

| | The moment | What the dealer needs |
|---|---|---|
| **M1** | They have won, or are bidding, a cinema — the project exists on paper, the room does not | The acoustics specified as part of the design |
| **M2** | They are pricing the build | A treatment number they can quote a client with — **the box quantum is what makes an early number possible** |
| **M3** | An architect or client has asked for a spec they cannot produce | The acoustic layer of someone else's paperwork |

**And `PR-7` is broken properly this time, on its own stated falsifier.** *It read "three problems means three
doors", with "evidence that a dealer does not arrive with a recognisable symptom" as what would break it — **which
is exactly what `PR-14` is.** I withdrew an earlier break of `PR-7` and was right to: I had defended it on the
anti-ranking point, which is `Q50`'s and is untouched. **The count of three survives** (Neil, `Q71`: *"three
stand"*); **what it is keyed to does not.*** *So: three doors, keyed on `M1`–`M3`. The 3 Rs is what the design
addresses once a dealer is inside — it is the design system (`Q70`), not the doormat.*

## Slot 4's job changed, and it changes all three doors

**It was the *proof* slot: "remove the last doubt — third-party, measured, verifiable."** **`PR-4` broke that, and
Neil put it plainly:** *"if it's not legal or safety no one gives a shit. Hardly any dealer cares about Sabines or
anything. All the others are the same — confirm it's not just made up, but don't go mad that you have some tests
either."*

**So `S4` confirms rather than proves.** *Its job is to show the claim is not invented, and then get out of the
way.* **Two consequences:**

- **It stops being the slot the journey turns on.** *The content plan's proof-first ordering was built on `PR-4`
  and should be re-read.*
- **The one place measurement is load-bearing is a compliance gate** — reaction-to-fire — **and that is not in the
  eight slots at all.** *It is `T1` in `training.md`, and it is the only subject where a dealer cannot finish the
  job without what we hold.*

## What the rows show that three documents could not

- **Four of eight slots were identical**, restated three times. **The doors differ in half as many places as the
  structure implied.**
- **`S1` is empty on two of three doors and dead on the third.** *One live hook, going nowhere. The entry stage is
  the emptiest part of the whole system, and it is now also the stage whose framing changed (`M1`–`M3`).*
- **`S7` is the reverse of what the record assumed.** *The door with a **real** predictable failure (REV-CP's
  cold-room adhesive) has its one-pager written; the door with **no** trap (reflection) had the most material; and
  the door whose placement genuinely decides whether the product works at all (resonance, away from a corner it is
  "a much weaker device") **has never had its page drafted.***
- **Nothing here is blocked on authoring.** *Of twelve per-door cells: two are paste jobs, four are publish-or-surface
  jobs, three are recordings, one is a fetch (`DOC-4`), one is an inventory check, and **one is a genuine piece of
  writing** — the corner-placement page. `PR-2`'s count holds up.*
