# C-ATS — the two growth levers

The buyer journey (`../../group/02-buyer-journey.md`) converts and retains a dealer regardless of
channel. Growth is about **which channels C-ATS aims it at.** Two levers:

**Lever 1 — win more in the residential installation channels we already know.** We know these firms
(`positioning.md`) and the content, proof and Experience Centre are already aimed at them. This is
the nearer-term, lower-risk lever: win more of the known residential AV / home-cinema integrators,
and — through the buyer journey and group cross-sell — win more from each. Deepening, not widening.

**Lever 2 — pursue channels that are new to us.** Adjacent buyers the same acoustics proposition
serves but that C-ATS doesn't currently reach:
- **Pro install** — recording/mastering studios and other performance-audio spaces. More demanding
  on raw acoustic data, less on "hidden behind fabric"; Isolation and Verification travel well here.
- **Commercial cinema** — boutique commercial screening rooms. This is where the in-development
  commercial range (`product-pricing.md`) points — same 3 Rs, adjusted specs. It's the product that
  opens the channel (don't market it until it ships — brand `CLAUDE.md`).
- **Fit-out companies** — high-end interior fit-out / construction firms who build these rooms. More
  a channel/multiplier than an end-buyer: a route to many rooms at once, likely a spec-influencer
  relationship rather than a straight dealer one.

Lever 2 is widening, and higher-uncertainty: each new channel is a different buyer with different
proof needs and objections, so each needs its own validation before spend — same flag-don't-guess
discipline as the residential buyer-truth. Don't assume the residential approach transfers wholesale.

Both levers share **the same buyer journey and the same content operation
(`../../group/04-content.md`).** A new channel mostly needs the proposition re-pointed
and channel-specific proof, not a separate operation — which is what makes lever 2 affordable to
try.

> **Group vs brand.** The idea of deepening known channels versus pursuing adjacent ones over one
> shared content operation is reusable and could generalise to any brand; the *specific channels and
> their buyers* here are C-ATS's, and stay brand-owned.
