# C-ATS — the claims, atomised

*The drilled-down form of `positioning.md`. **One row per atomic claim**, each with who confirmed it and when.
`positioning.md` is 249 lines of prose with **zero attributions to Neil** — every word of it was drafted by a
prior session and then treated as settled strategy. This file is where that gets undone one claim at a time.*

**Why atomised** (Neil, 2026-08-18): *"1 paragraph became 15 data points! …this is exactly the power of drilling
down."* A paragraph can be waved through as "correct" while carrying a wrong claim inside it. A row cannot.

## How it runs

- **ID is stable.** `C<section>.<n>`, never reused, never renumbered.
- **Kind** says which file owns the claim: **`brand`** (belongs here) · **`product`** (belongs in
  `product-records.md` — a row marked product is a **duplication to remove**, not a claim to keep) ·
  **`service`** · **`gap`** (named in the prose, described nowhere) · **`discussion`**.
- **`discussion` is the kind that matters most, and it was added on section 2** (Neil, 2026-08-18): *"this is an
  example of a discussion being placed as core product truth"*, *"again just grossly inflated conversation."*
  **It is not a weak claim — it is reasoning that was never a claim at all**, written up in the register's own
  voice and then inherited as settled. `../../method.md` failure 2 names it: *inference must not wear the voice
  of measurement*. **A `discussion` row is struck, not softened**, and what replaces it is the owner's plain
  sentence, which is always shorter.
- **Status:** `confirmed` (with date — Neil's own words, or his explicit yes) · `drafted` (unconfirmed, and the
  default for everything inherited from `positioning.md`) · `open` (a specific question raised and not yet
  answered) · `struck`.
- **A claim is not confirmed because the paragraph containing it was.** Where Neil confirmed a passage as a
  whole, the rows say so and any point he singled out keeps its own status.

### The two answers, and why they are not in tension

**Q59 — the two-route choice is aspiration.** *"Aspiration, not truth — mark it."* The promise leads with a
self-serve route that has no mechanism behind it. **It is not struck** — it is what C-ATS intends — but it stops
being stated as fact until DR-Q52 settles what publishes and something carries it. **This is `../../CLAUDE.md`'s
build-it-then-say-it arriving in the promise itself**, which is the most expensive place for it to be: the
promise is the sentence a dealer is asked to believe.

**Q60 — "measured and proven, every time" holds.** *"It holds — it's about capability."* Verification never
having been invoiced does not weaken it, because **the claim is that the result *can* be measured and proven,
which is true and rare in acoustics.** *I had proposed tightening it as an overclaim; that was wrong, and the
distinction is worth keeping: a capability claim and a delivery claim look identical in prose and are not the
same thing.* **C3.4 is the strongest true sentence in the document.**

### C4.10, and what it costs

**Neil, 2026-08-18 (Q61): the credibility is *"the systemised method — that's the real answer."*** Not the
lineage. **The claim is that the design method is written down, encoded and reproducible** — per-axis Fitzroy
flatness, surface-palette matching, reflection triage against a binding budget, corner loading, validated on a
real room — **not that we have been doing it since 2014.** Longevity is context; the system is the claim.

**Three consequences, and the third is uncomfortable:**

1. **`PR-4` breaks.** The premise that BSRIA data is C-ATS's strongest proof asset carried the proof slot on all
   three pathways and the proof-first ordering of the content plan. **The data stays real, published and
   free — but it is not what the credibility rests on.** The method is.
2. **C4.10 was written as a guess and turned out to be right for reasons its author did not have.** The file says
   *"a design lineage going back to 2014"*; the actual evidence — `c-ats-system-design-rules.md` and the engine —
   was in another repo and had never been read from here.
3. **C-ATS's credibility claim is currently unpublishable.** If credibility is the systemised method, and the
   method is black-boxed as IP, **then the strongest thing about the brand is the thing it may not show.** That
   raises DR-Q52 from a content question to the central one for this brand.

### A date to settle

C4.3 says *"more than a decade"*; C4.10's reasoning says *"a design lineage going back to 2014"* — consistent at
twelve years. But the ledger shows C-ATS product revenue from **2015** and the BSRIA test is **2019**. **Which
date the credibility claim rests on is unasked**, and it is one sentence to an owner.
