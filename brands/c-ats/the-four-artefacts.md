## What the live site actually says, and three things follow

**`G4` is confirmed exactly as the record states it — and this is the repo's most under-used asset.** *The technical
page carries **the full BSRIA report** (`BSRIA-Final-Report-100241-1.pdf`, March 2020) and **five coefficient
sheets** (REF-CP · RES-CP · REF-CP-MAR · REV-CP-50 · **the resonance boundary condition**), all April 2023, all
freely downloadable with no gate.* **Specifier-grade third-party proof, public for years.**

**1. `DOC-8` is probably answerable from a document already public.** *The page states the commission plainly:
**"tests were conducted on 3 materials in 8 different configurations in accordance with BS EN ISO 354:2003."*** *So
the **eight BSRIA tests** the record cites (tests 3, 4, 5, 6, 8…) are **one commission**, and **the marine sheet was
published in the same April 2023 batch as the other four**. **Three materials against four panel sheets suggests the
marine panel is the same material as the REF-CP** — which is what `DOC-8` asks. **Derivable from the published
report; not yet verified, so it stays a question, not an answer.***

**2. `DAT-1`'s blast radius is wider than the dataset.** *The live site says **"all of this is achieved within a
depth of just 50mm"** — the whole-system claim. **The RES-CP is 43 mm** (Neil, 2026-08-19). *So the wrong depth is in
`panels.json` **and on the live site**, and it has been for years.* **`DAT-1` was scoped as a data fix; it is also a
content fix on a live page.**

**3. `C1.22` is true of the live site, and for a different reason than `PR-18` recorded.** *`C1.22` says C-ATS shows
**no services at all**. `PR-18` explained that away — the site being read was *"an in-play mockup that has not listed
them yet."* **But the live site genuinely has no services**: no design service, no verification, no isolation, on a
page unchanged for years. *`C1.22` stands, and its **real** basis is the live site rather than the mockup's
incompleteness.*

## Two things on the live site that would not pass the current rules

- **Unbacked superlatives.** *"**a unique** acoustical treatment system designed to offer the **maximum performance**
  with the **minimum loss of space** across all frequencies."* **Three in one sentence**, against the group's voice
  rule — *no superlative without a measurement behind it* — and against `Q63`, which bounded exactly this kind of
  depth claim. *Note the shape: **`copy.md` carries the same instinct** ("shallower than anything comparable"), so
  this is not a legacy accident, it is a habit the record has been correcting.*
- **Nothing says the product is trade-only.** *The live site reads as a product brochure with a contact form.
  `commercial-model.md` and the record both have C-ATS as **trade only, `srnd.store`** — the newer artefacts say so
  in their footers and **the live one does not.***

*And one thing it gets right that the legacy brochure did not: **the word "diffuser" does not appear.** The
brochure's *"faceted ABS diffuser"* never reached the site.*
