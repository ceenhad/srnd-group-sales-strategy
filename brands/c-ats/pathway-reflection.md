## The door

The problem, in the dealer's words: **`[?]`**. Drafted phrasing exists — *"the sound arriving off the side walls
competes with the sound from the speakers; detail smears"* (`copy.md`) — **and it is ours, not a dealer's**
(`product-records.md` `O1` — **unasked, not blocked**). The product: the REF-CP, a
low-value, low-risk first order — 300 × 300 × 50 mm, 16 to a box — which is what the journey asks of a door.

**A door is a way in, not a rank.** C-ATS is a system and the 3 Rs is what defines it; no panel leads and none is
the entry product (Neil, 2026-08-18, `../../registers/questions.md` Q50). Three doors exist because there are
three problems, not because there is an order to work through them in.

**One thing is different about this door and it shapes every slot below.** The reverberation door sells on a
number that looks good. **This one sells on numbers that look bad**: the REF-CP's absorption coefficients are
deliberately low, because it is a scatterer and not an absorber (`product-records.md` §1). A dealer reading the
published data without the explanation reads it as a weak product. **So the proof slot on this pathway has to
teach before it can prove**, and that is the pathway's real work.

## What writing it down shows

- **The template holds.** Eight slots, same shape, different substance. Nothing about the second door needed a
  new slot, and slot 5 turned out to be *shared* rather than duplicated — one hinge serves every door, which is
  worth knowing before three pathways each specify their own registration piece.
- **The proof slot is not symmetrical between doors.** On door 1 the data is the proof. Here the data needs an
  explainer in front of it or it argues against us. **A pathway can have a proof asset that is published, free,
  third-party — and still net negative until the teaching piece exists.** That is a general finding, not a C-ATS
  one.
- **This door's centre of gravity is slot 7, not slot 1.** The single most valuable piece on the whole pathway is
  an install one-pager that is already written and unpublished. **A door can be worth opening for what happens
  after the order rather than before it** — and on the ledger, that is where C-ATS loses people
  (`segments.md`: 36 of 53 buyers ordered in one year only).
- **The register items this pathway consumes, in order:** CON-8 (the handoffs), publication of the staged KB
  articles, **DOC-4** (read the A/B values off the chart — it blocks the teaching piece that unblocks the proof
  slot), EST-5, EST-4, DOC-1, DOC-2, DOC-3. Nothing new gets authored except the fault-finding one-pager and the
  adjacency page.
- **Two exits, not one.** Door 1 lands on Fabric Walls. This one lands on Fabric Walls *or* DT depending on what
  is being built, which makes it the better door to open on a dealer who already buys projection — and per
  `segments.md` that is 142 accounts.

## Guardrails (brand truth binds, hardest here)

**Scattering, never diffusion** — the word is not to be used, and our own legacy pack brochure gets it wrong
("faceted ABS diffuser"), which is a source to fix rather than a phrasing to copy. **No scattering-coefficient
claim** — ISO 17497 data has never been measured. Panels named by problem, never mechanism. **C-ATS**, never the
expansion. Every figure is published BSRIA data or the estate's own counts. The marine variant is a compliance
edge case and gets no segment-level prominence. The dealer's phrasing in "the door" stays flagged until the
archive supplies it.
