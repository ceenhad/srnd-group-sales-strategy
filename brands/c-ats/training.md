## What this instance shows about the leg

**Rewritten 2026-08-21: all five rows are now runnable, and none of them needed new information.**

- **Five rows, five runnable.** *`T5` and `T2` were drafted on 2026-08-19. On 2026-08-21 `T1` was drafted, `T3`'s
  shape and fee were settled (`Q89`, `Q94`), and `T4` was un-gated (`Q90`, `gating-sort.md`).* **Nothing in the leg was
  ever blocked on research.** *Each block turned out to be a decision nobody had been asked for, or a belief about the
  record that reading the primary source dissolved.*
- **The compliance row is the strongest, exactly as the rule predicts — and it was blocked on a wrong description of
  its own blocker.** *`T1` was carried as waiting on one missing EN 13501-1 classification. The fire position is **five
  positions across four materials**, and the piece's most useful section — in a concealed system the exposed lining
  surface is the fabric — was in nobody's notes. **The row that looked most blocked had the most behind it.***
- **The specifier cast now has two rows, not one.** *`T2` was the only one. `N7` joined it as an artefact for the same
  reader (`Q76`), and `T1`'s fabric-rating section speaks to whoever specifies the finish. Given `C2.27` and a
  competitor running three ISE sessions at architects and interior designers, **that ratio was the finding and it has
  moved.***
- **The gating question turned out to be an asset, not an obstacle.** *`DR-Q52` blocked two rows for weeks. `Q90`
  supplied the test — product fact publishes, anything advancing the sale gates — and the sort found the strongest
  material on the public side. **`T1` then used the same gate on a weakness rather than a sales item** (`Q93`), which
  is a structure nobody had proposed.*
- **What every row now waits on is an owner's calendar**, which `Q25` says is Neil's and Simon's. *That is the honest
  end state for this leg: not a syllabus, and not a queue of research — five subjects that need someone to stand up and
  deliver them.*
