or a confirmed claim** — a subject with no trace is a topic, not an opportunity. **Not yet put to Neil**; the
*"needs to run"* column is mostly an owner's calendar, which `Q25` says is his and Simon's.*

**The rule this file was built under** (Neil, 2026-08-19): ***"If it's not legal or safety no one gives a shit.

their job**, never by displaying what we have measured. *One exception, and it is the strongest row below: a
compliance gate, where a dealer cannot finish the job without the certificate.*

## What this instance shows about the leg

- **Five rows, and only one is runnable today** (`T5`) — with a second (`T2`) runnable as a class and blocked only
  on accreditation for the route that reaches architects. *The rest wait on a fetch (`T1`), a price (`Q45`, `T3`) or
  a decision elsewhere (`DR-Q52`, `T4`).* **The leg's value is that it made the queue visible, not that it produced
  a syllabus.**
- **The compliance row is the strongest, exactly as the rule predicts.** *`T1` is the only subject where a dealer
  **cannot complete the job** without what we know — and it is blocked on a single missing classification in our own
  record.*
- **Only `T2` reaches the specifier cast.** *Four of five rows speak to the integrator. Given `C2.27` and the
  finding that a competitor's partner runs three ISE sessions aimed at architects and interior designers, **that
  ratio is the finding.***
- **Two rows are gated by decisions this repo does not own** (`DR-Q52`) **and one by a question it does** (`Q45`).
  *`Q45` has been open since 2026-08-17; it now blocks a training subject as well as a price.*
