# C-ATS — the segments, and which pathway each one enters

*The third leg of the template run (`../../NEXT.md` item 2). The measured cut is
`../../evidence/2026-08-18-cats-segment-cut.md`; the per-account table is `../../data/derived/cats-segments.csv`.
**This file does not restate the numbers — it says what each segment is and where it enters the journey**, so a
segment maps to a pathway slot rather than to a mailing list. Drafted for correction. Other brands copy the shape.*

> **A segment is a state, not a rank** (Neil, 2026-08-18, `../../registers/questions.md` Q51): *"we are treating
> every relationship as day 1 startup."* **Nothing here orders dealers by what they have spent.** What a segment
> records is what a dealer has already been shown — which decides what they need next, and nothing else. The first
> draft of this file used the ledger as a league table; that was an account-value logic the strategy does not hold,
> and it is struck.

## The finding that decides the shape

**Nine dealers in ten who buy from SRND have never bought a C-ATS panel.** 53 accounts have ever bought it,
against 549 in the group ledger — and 142 of those bought something else from us in 2024–26.

That changes what the brand's content has to do, not who is worth talking to. **A dealer who already buys from us
has met the group; they have not met this problem.** The journey says exactly what that means: an existing dealer
crossing to a second brand *re-enters at stage 2, not stage 1* — they do not need the hook, they need the
proposition and the proof (`../../motion/motion-design.md`, "the loop … re-enters at stage 2"). **So the largest
part of the field skips G1 entirely**, which is the one gateway the brand's content is currently weighted toward.

**It puts a count on an adjacency the map already holds.** `../../motion/adjacency-map.md` (XS-1, written
2026-08-14) already lists DT → C-ATS and Fabric Walls → C-ATS as entry points, with the project moment for each.
What was missing was how much field there is: 142 accounts. The reverberation pathway exits onto Fabric Walls at
its stage 2 (`pathway-reverberation.md` slot 8); the ledger says traffic runs the other way too. **So the C-ATS
adjacency page (`content-plan.md` item 45) is an entrance as well as an exit** — written once, read from both ends.

## Three things this changes about the plan

- **The install layer is aimed at the wrong count.** Thirty-six of 53 buyers bought C-ATS in exactly one year.
  Whatever else is true, **G4 → G5 → G6 is where the brand loses people**, and the install pack
  (`content-plan.md` item 50) is the piece pointed at it. It is drafted, and its inputs — items 1, 2, 37–39 — are
  written or one session's work.
- **The permissioned channel is 191 people.** That is every contact with `Subscribe C-ATS = Yes`, against 985 who
  subscribe at group level. The direct half of the motion cannot reach more than that today. **Growing it is a
  stage-3 job, not a mail job** — permission is given at the hinge, in exchange for something worth crossing for.
- **The CRM cannot currently answer this question.** It marks 20 accounts as current or historic C-ATS customers;
  the ledger shows 53. Nine contacts carry `C-ATS Relationship = Dealer`. **Until the account × brand signal
  matrix lands in engine, the ledger is the only honest source of who is a C-ATS dealer** — an argument for the
  matrix, not a criticism of Monday.

## What is not decided here, and must not be guessed

- **Why the 142 have not bought** — not in any ledger. `../../registers/backlog.md` CON-3.
- **In what order to approach anybody.** The cut supports no running order, and per Q51 it is not to be used to
  invent one. Room type, whether a dealer already buys acoustics elsewhere, and whether they were ever asked are
  all unknown, and they are the things that would actually decide it.
- **Whether the lapsed 16 lapsed for a reason.** Several bought once in 2021 and never again. Nothing here says
  whether that is a product problem, a price problem, or nobody asking.
- **The 191 subscribers' overlap with the 53 buyers** — the join is on contact, and the ledger has no contact
  column, only an account. That is an engine question, not a spreadsheet one.

## Guardrails

**Internal only.** Named dealer relationships and invoice-level revenue do not appear in any published piece, in
any form (`../../data/README.md`, sensitivity). Segments inform which pathway a dealer is offered — they are never
described back to the dealer, and no piece of content refers to a segment.

## The entry re-key, and the limit it exposes

*Added 2026-08-19, closing `MTH-1`. **Entry is now keyed to the project moment** — `M1` won or bidding, `M2` pricing
the build, `M3` an architect has asked for a spec (`pathways.md`, `claims.md` `C2.24`–`C2.26`). **This table cannot
see any of them.***

**A segment says what a dealer has already been shown. A moment says what they are doing this week.** *The two are
orthogonal, and **nothing in the ledger or the CRM records a project stage** — no bid, no design-stage flag, no
quote-in-progress. So the cut can route a dealer to a **stage** of the journey and cannot route them to a
**moment**.*

**Three consequences, and the first is the one that matters.**

1. **The moment is discovered in conversation, or not at all.** *Which makes it a question the first exchange has to
   answer — "what stage is the project at" — rather than something segmentation can pre-empt. **It is also why `M2`
   being the worst-served moment (`hooks.md`) cannot be seen in any number here.***
2. **It does not weaken the cut, it bounds it.** *The segments still say what a dealer has seen, which is what they
   were built to say (`Q51`: a fact about their state, never a ranking). **The re-key changes what the content says
   at entry, not who is at the door.***
3. **It is the same instrument gap as engagement.** *`G2` recorded that engagement cannot be measured until the
   inbound log exists. **Project stage is a second field nothing captures**, and both would be captured in the same
   place — engine, at the point of contact (`../../operations/engine-as-hub.md`).*

**What this does not license.** *Guessing a moment from a segment. **A dormant account is not therefore at `M1`, and
a group-active dealer is not therefore pricing a build** — the mapping does not exist and inventing it would be the
league-table error (`Q51`) in a new costume.*
