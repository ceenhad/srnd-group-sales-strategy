## 4. Datasheet / product-page paragraph — site conditions

> **Site conditions.** Install with the room above 18 °C, held for 24 hours. Substrate clean, dry and
> dust-free. A single 25 mm retention screw per panel is recommended wherever the panel will be concealed
> behind fabric, on any ceiling, and in buildings still drying out.
