## 1. Canonical article — `cinema-expert.com`, Instructions

**Title (the installer's question):** *Why do self-adhesive acoustic panels let go weeks after the install —
and how do you stop it?*

The body is the source page essentially as it stands — its own footer says it can go out without figures.
What the canonical version adds is the frame and the handoff:

> The adhesive backing on the Reverberation Control Panel needs a warm, dry, clean substrate. **The room
> must be above 18 °C, held for 24 hours, before installation** — not warmed for an hour beforehand. A cold
> wall takes the panel and releases it weeks later, usually after the fabric has gone on. This is the single
> most preventable failure in the C-ATS range, and it is preventable only before the fact.
>
> *(Then the source page's How to fit it / When to use the screw / If a panel has already let go, verbatim.)*

**Handoff:** the Reverberation Control Panel product page → the install guide → "planning a room? The design

## 4. Datasheet / product-page paragraph — site conditions

> **Site conditions.** Install with the room above 18 °C, held for 24 hours. Substrate clean, dry and
> dust-free. A single 25 mm retention screw per panel is recommended wherever the panel will be concealed
> behind fabric, on any ceiling, and in buildings still drying out.
