## Page 1 — Reflection Control Panel: bond it, don't just screw it

### The short version

**Fix the Reflection Control Panel with adhesive as well as screws.** Screws alone change how the panel behaves
acoustically. It will look identical and it will not perform as designed.

### Why it matters

The Reflection Control Panel is a **scatterer**, not an absorber. Its job is to take the reflection off a side
wall, rear wall or ceiling and send it away from the seat, while leaving the room's energy intact.

- **Bonded (Type A — correct).** The adhesive holds the centre of the panel and damps it. The panel stays rigid, so
  it does what it is designed to do: redirect. Absorption stays low, which is the intent.
- **Screwed only (Type B).** The panel is free to flex and adds an absorption peak of its own. It absorbs *more* —
  which sounds like an improvement and is not, because the acoustic design assumed it would not.

Both behaviours are real and both are published on the absorption chart as **Reflection A** and **Reflection B**.
A is the specified condition.

### How to fix it

1. Apply builder's adhesive to the centre of the panel back. Centre coverage is what matters, not full coverage.
2. Locate the panel and press it home.
3. Fit **4 × 3.5 × 25 mm bugle-head screws** through the moulded countersunk holes.
4. Repeat. Position tolerance is generous — see below.

### Position tolerance

Panels may sit **up to about 300 mm from the designed position** without materially changing the result. Rooms
rarely match drawings; work to the layout, not to the millimetre.

### If you have already installed without adhesive

The room will measure differently from the design, most noticeably in the mid band. It is not a defect and nothing
needs replacing — but tell us before commissioning so the design can be checked against what was actually built.

## Page 2 — Reverberation Control Panel: the room must be warm before you fit it

### The short version

**The room must be above 18 °C for 24 hours before installation.** The adhesive backing is temperature-sensitive.
Fit it cold and it will hold at first and let go later.

### Why it matters

The Reverberation Control Panel is supplied with a pre-applied adhesive backing so it can be fitted quickly and
without fixings. That backing needs a warm, dry, clean substrate to bond properly. A cold wall — a room still
drying out, a winter build with the heating not yet commissioned — will take the panel and release it weeks later,
usually after the fabric has gone on.

**This is the single most preventable failure in the C-ATS range**, and it is preventable only before the fact.

### How to fit it

1. **Condition the room:** above 18 °C, held for 24 hours. Not warmed for an hour beforehand.
2. Check the substrate is clean, dry and dust-free.
3. Peel and position. Press firmly across the whole panel.
4. **Optional permanent insurance:** one 25 mm screw per panel. Drive it about two thirds in, then reverse it until
   the dimple disappears. The screw holds; the surface stays flat.

### When to use the screw

Use it wherever the panel will be concealed behind fabric, on any ceiling, and on any job where the building is
still drying. A screw costs seconds. A panel that drops behind a finished fabric wall costs a return visit and the
client's confidence.

### If a panel has already let go

It is an adhesive failure, not a panel failure. Refit with adhesive and a screw. If several have gone, the room was
probably cold at installation — worth knowing before the rest follow.
