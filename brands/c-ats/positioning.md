# C-ATS — positioning

Cinema acoustics brand for **small-room cinema acoustics** — dedicated home cinemas, media rooms,
boutique screening rooms, DCI screening rooms. Over a decade trading. The engineering brief is the
**3 Rs** (Reflection / Resonance / Reverberation); the product range and the site are both organised
around it.

C-ATS is **one brand in the SRND group** — see `../../group/01-dealer-as-asset.md` for why that
changes the economics. The buyer is the AV integrator ("the dealer"). End clients occasionally buy
direct (by enquiry — no pricing is published to anyone, per the group pricing policy), but
everything optimises for the dealer's journey.

Today's core is high-end residential; the growth plan explicitly reaches adjacent channels too
(`growth-levers.md`). C-ATS transacts through the shared SRND store on the group's
direct-to-dealer, global, portal-led model (`../../group/02-commercial-model.md`) — not
distributor-led.

**Starting line — be honest about it.** The dedicated C-ATS front-end content is largely staged, not
launched; the brand currently appears on the shared store as a brand page
(`/pages/complete-acoustic-treatment-system`) plus its collections. So C-ATS's instance of the
wheel isn't fully turning yet. This is a *build*, not an optimisation.

## The 3 Rs (the engineering brief, not a marketing device)

- **Reflection** → Reflection Control Panel (REF-CP)
- **Resonance** → Resonance Control Panel (RES-CP)
- **Reverberation** → Reverberation Control Panel (REV-CP-50)

One panel type per acoustic problem. Products named by the problem they solve, never the mechanism.
The depth advantage (headline ~50mm) is real but must not be overclaimed — state the benefit, never
a performance figure not backed by the technical data. Full brand truth: `ceenhad/c-ats-shopify:CLAUDE.md`.
