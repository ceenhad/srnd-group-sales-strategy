# C-ATS — the reverberation pathway (the first, and the template)

*The first content pathway written end to end (`../../group/09-motion-design.md`; `../../decided.md` S22;
register JNY-2). One entry door: the dealer whose client's room sounds wrong — reverberation — and the panel
that answers it, the **REV-CP**. Chosen because every slot's material either exists or is already written, so
this is **assembly, not creation**. Each slot names the piece, its one job, its honest state, and the handoff
that connects it to the next — the handoffs are the pathway. Drafted for correction. Once corrected, this is
the template every other door copies: the slots generalise, only the substance changes.*

## The door

The problem, in the dealer's words: *"the room sounds echoey — you can't make out the dialogue"* **[phrasing
to confirm from the archive count, CON-3 — the dealer's own words are the search terms and must come from
real questions, not be invented]**. The product: the REV-CP. A low-value, low-risk first order — boxes of
panels, easy to specify, quick to prove — which is exactly what the journey asks of a door
(`../../group/02-buyer-journey.md`: the door is a product, not a brand).

## What writing it down shows

- **Assembly, not creation.** Seven of the eight slots are written, staged, live-but-buried, or an existing
  register fix. The only genuinely missing piece is the adjacency prompt (XS-1 — one page). The stage-2
  break, the worst gap the journey map found, turns out to be a paste job.
- **The dead ends are the pathway's whole cost.** Every piece exists more than the connections do. Slot 1 —
  the best-performing content the group has ever made — currently hands to nothing at all.
- **The register items this pathway consumes, in order:** CON-8 (the handoffs), publication of the staged KB
  articles it passes through, EST-5, EST-4, DOC-1, DOC-2, DOC-3, XS-1. Completing them *is* completing the
  pathway; nothing new gets authored except the adjacency page.
- **The loop lands on Fabric Walls at its stage 2** — so the Fabric Walls proposition layer is the next
  pathway to write, and its strongest asset already exists.

## Guardrails (brand truth binds, hardest here)

Every figure above is published BSRIA data or the estate's own view counts — nothing invented. Panels named
by problem, never mechanism. **C-ATS**, never the expansion. No teasing the commercial range. The design
service is the dealer's choice, never a rescue. The dealer's phrasing in "the door" stays flagged until the
archive supplies it.
