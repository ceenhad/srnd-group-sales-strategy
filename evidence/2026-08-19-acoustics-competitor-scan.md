## Two facts worth recording on their own

**Both published absorption figures exceed 1.0.** GIK's headline is **NRC=1.05**; Primacoustic's 2″ panel reads
**1.07 at 500 Hz**. *A coefficient above unity is a known artefact of the reverberation-room method — edge
diffraction and finite sample area — not necessarily a claim of impossible absorption. Recorded as observed; the
interpretation belongs elsewhere.*

**GIK and C-ATS cite the same standard.** BS EN ISO 354:2003 — GIK at the University of Salford, C-ATS at BSRIA
Bracknell (`../brands/c-ats/product-records.md`). **The standard is not a differentiator between them; what is
published against it differs.**
