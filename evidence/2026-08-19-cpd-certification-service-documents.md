## 1. Which route this is — and it is the cheaper one, not the RIBA one

**This is route (b) in the platform's own plan** *(`2026-08-10-accreditation-and-cpd-routes.md`, and see
`2026-08-19-the-four-revenue-lines-platform-read.md` §4)*, which described commercial accreditors as issuing **"a
badge; they do not carry specifier distribution… useful as a stamp on the online modules, not a route to
architects."*

**The two are not alternatives and both can run:**

| | **The CPD Certification Service** (these documents) | **RIBA CPD Providers Network** |
|---|---|---|
| What you get | The **'CPD Certified'** trade mark on approved materials and on delegate certificates; a Member Directory entry | Content assessed against the **Core Curriculum**, plus **distribution** — NBS Source, the CPD Showcase, RIBA events |
| Reach | The badge travels wherever we put it | **Puts us in front of architects who must find 35 hours a year** |
| Best used for | **A stamp on the Cinema Expert online modules** | **The seminar route to specifiers** |

**A correction to the platform plan's figures, from the form in hand.** *The plan put commercial accreditors at
**"£150–£1,600 per course, or £200–£500/yr for a package."*** **The actual quote is a `MEMBER(5) ORGANISATION`
membership at £2,250 + VAT for two years** *(£2,700 inc VAT, UK; £2,250 no VAT for Europe & International, with a
two-year discount applied).* **So the package figure in the plan is low by roughly a factor of two.** *Per unit it is
still cheap — if the `(5)` is a five-certification allowance, that is about **£225 per certification per year** — but
**`(5)`'s meaning is an inference from the terms**, which describe membership categories by the number of
certifications carried forward. **Confirm it on the consultation call rather than assuming it.**

## 2. What certification actually covers — and what it does not

- **It certifies the materials, not us.** *The terms are explicit: **"CPD certification covers your CPD Materials
  only and not your business, organisation or personnel."*** **So this is not a company accreditation and must never
  be described as one.**
- **The trade marks are licensed, narrowly.** *`'The CPD Certification Service'`, `'CPD Certified'` and `'CPD Member'`
  are theirs; we get a **non-exclusive, revocable, non-transferable** licence for the term, usable on **one website
  we own or control** — more sites need prior written approval — and on social platforms.*
- **Materials must be kept current or certification is withdrawn.** *They reserve the right to **withdraw
  certification or require resubmission** where materials are not kept up to date.* **The same two-year refresh
  discipline the RIBA Quality Code imposes, so it is one obligation, not two.**
- **They publish us.** *Member name, logo and certified activities go on `cpduk.co.uk` unless we notify otherwise.*
- **Turnaround is 10–14 working days** per assessment, *"depending on the size and duration of the educational
  activity"* — **a real planning number, and the first hard lead time in the whole education layer.**
- *Also included: a **delegate certificate service**, which removes the post-training certificate admin.*

## 3. The rule that decides everything, stated more bluntly than RIBA states it

> ***"Educational, information-led and value added learning – no products & service selling. Promote your service
> through expertise and knowledge provision using best practice guidelines."***

**And a concrete constraint that follows: *"branding should be limited to opening and closing slides."***

**So the no-pitch discipline is now stated by both routes**, and it is no longer a house preference: *it is an
assessment criterion in one case and a Quality Code requirement in the other.* **`../CLAUDE.md`'s "never lead with our
own brilliance" turns out to be the entry condition for the channel**, which is worth noticing — *the voice rule and
the commercial route agree, and neither was derived from the other.*

## 4. What a submission has to contain — and this is where our drafts fall short

**A seminar is the right shape for `T2`.** *Their definition: **a few hours up to one day, focused on one subject,
group setting of no more than 20, with group work and interactive discussion.** That is exactly what `T2` is.*

**Required for a seminar submission — eight items:**

| # | Required | Do we have it? |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Completed Submission for CPD Certification form | **No** — comes with membership |
| 2 | **Timed agenda**, showing start, end and comfort breaks | **No** |
| 3 | **Presenter instruction notes** — room layout, handouts | **No** |
| 4 | **Presentation with speaker notes**, branding on opening and closing slides only | **No** |
| 5 | **Delegate feedback mechanism**, and handouts if any | **No** |
| 6 | Delegate evaluation, if offered *(knowledge tests need every question, the correct answers and any pass/fail criteria)* | **No** |
| 7–8 | File delivery and chronological labelling | n/a |

**The honest position on `T2` and `T5`, therefore: they are the *argument*, not the submission.** *What exists is the
content and the traceability. **What a CPD submission needs is the teaching apparatus around it** — agenda, speaker
notes, handout, feedback form, and a learning-objectives frame at the front.*

**Their "getting it right first time" list is effectively the missing frame, and it is short:**

- **Clear learning objectives, outcomes and a summary of key information *from the start*** — *"Why am I here? What
  will I learn?"* **Neither draft opens this way; both open with the claim.**
- **Examples, case studies, facts and stats to put the information in context.** *`T2` has none — and `product-records.md`
  `N8` says a decade of cinema work and the Cornflake IMAX install are captured as **nothing**. **That gap is now
  blocking a channel, not just a content plan.***
- **Participation** — *Q&A, quiz, assessment, discussion or reflective notes.* **`T2`'s three-refusals section is a
  natural discussion prompt** *(which of the three happens on your projects?)* — **a room of architects answering that
  is also the answer to `N6`'s `[?]`, which nobody has ever collected.**
- **A methodical learning journey, beginner to expert**, and **a feedback loop** to improve the activity.

## 5. What this changes here

1. **`T2`'s route question is settled twice over and the work is now apparatus, not argument.** *`training.md` `T2`
   and `T5` get a *"what a submission needs"* line rather than being called runnable-as-is. **Still runnable as our
   own class today** — that needs nobody's approval and no membership.*
2. **The participation requirement turns a class into a data-collection instrument.** *A seminar that asks *"which of
   the three refusals happens on your projects?"* answers `N6`'s open `[?]` — **when the acoustic decision actually
   gets made** — from the people who make it. **That is worth more than the seminar.**
3. **`N8` — field learning — is now on the critical path.** *No case studies means no submission-grade material,
   for either route. `DOC-16`/`N8` were content-plan items; they are now channel blockers.*
4. **The two-year refresh obligation should be recorded once, wherever the material lives**, since both routes impose
   it.
