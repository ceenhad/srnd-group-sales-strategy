*Recovered 2026-08-01 from Neil's Dropbox ("BUYER JOURNEY ALL DOCS_MARCH2023", 34 files, dated 13 March
2023 — the month SRND Group was formed — with the RAPS files touched as late as April 2025). This is

## What the archive is

A complete buyer-journey programme built for **Apex-Tech UK** (rebranded from GT in July 2021) with an
external creative resource ("DZYN") and a direct sales team (Alan, Adrian): a consolidated strategy deck,
Neil's journey notes and directives, a purchase-decision question tree, the **RAPS** direct-sales process
(Research – Approach – Present – Secure) fully specified with per-step scripts and CRM discipline, and
awareness campaign templates.

> **Resolved (Neil, 2026-08-01):** Apex-Tech UK is effectively the **forerunner of SRND Distribution** —
> the group's own trade operation, GT → Apex-Tech UK (July 2021) → SRND Distribution. "We have been doing
> this a long time." Distinct from the *Apex-Tech* recorded in `../evidence/current-state.md` as the resigned US DT
> distributor; the two must not be conflated. Consequence: everything in this archive — RAPS, the CRM base,

## The 2023 journey — and how it maps to today's

Three states, with **explicitly defined boundaries** — Neil was defining gateways in 2023:

| 2023 | Sub-goals ("set in stone") | State boundary, as defined then |
|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** (indirect) | Know the company exists · know the solutions · know the application · know how to get in touch | **Ends when the buyer makes contact** |
| **Consideration** (direct) | Trust building · expertise building · how to apply · how to obtain | **Ends when the buyer requests a proposal — not when general pricing is supplied** |
| **Decision** (direct) | Post-sale assistance stated · a clear partner agreement · unambiguous pricing and payment terms · reliable updates and delivery | Assume a competing alternative offer, always |
| **The loop** | — | **"The loop should take them back to Consideration for the next project — looking at the project, not the product"** |

An earlier iteration in the same deck had **nine "bridges"** (Problem, Options, Engage, Qualify, Question,
Maxed Out, Certainty, Confidence, Ready) with nine matched "reach-outs" — the gateway idea in its first
form, superseded by the states table above in the deck's own words.

## What it validates (evidence for the ratchet)

- **S22 — the journey as a process with gateways.** Not a new idea: bridges (2023), explicit stage-end
  definitions, and "every company on our CRM is at one of these bridges right now — identify which, and get
  them to the next one." Three years old, in Neil's hand.
- **S23 — the appeal categories.** The *Purchase Events* mind-map's "What does it do" branch is the category
  set, near-verbatim, three years early: **solve a problem · save time · earn more revenue · a good
  experience** — each with its sub-questions ("what is the value of this time," "can I sample the
  experience"). And the awareness directive states the buyer's wants as **best performance, easiest way,
  maximum margin**. The categories are a held position, not a fresh brainstorm.
- **The buyer definition.** "Our buyer is a B2B dealer/installer… their client has already approached
  them… we are not interested in the client's journey to the dealer." Identical to the current strategy's
  buyer truth.
- **Channel doctrine.** Only channels where the recipient is known or reach is measurable; no print;
  LinkedIn "by far the most important B2B channel." The current online-first rule, already decided in 2023.
- **The known-base insight.** "Forget targeting anyone not on our CRM — if we can't sell to people who
  already know us, how do we sell to strangers?" The same logic as C1k (US entry is conversion, not
  acquisition).

## What it refines (candidate adoptions, for Neil to confirm)

1. **Two crisp boundary signals worth adopting into the gateway table** (JNY-1): *consideration begins at
   contact*; *decision begins at proposal request, explicitly not at pricing supplied*. Sharper than what
   the current G2/G4 rows say.
2. **The four awareness sub-goals as the test for every awareness piece**: which of the four does this
   advance — exists, solutions, application, contact? A finer instrument than "did it hook."
3. **The loop is per-project and re-enters at consideration.** Refines the current "loop re-enters at
   stage 2": the returning dealer isn't re-hooked, they arrive with the *next project* asking "how does
   this apply here."
4. **The Purchase Events tree is a ready-made doubt checklist** for the product data schema (groups 2–4):
   choice, training-vs-expert, price transparency ("my price — easy to calculate?"), standard-vs-bespoke,
   terms. Filled per product, it enumerates the questions the record must answer.
5. **RAPS is the missing process for "the ask."** The current model leaves "a very small number of people
   doing the asking" undesigned; RAPS *is* that design — research, approach, present, secure, with
   follow-up discipline (quotes chased inside ten days) and compulsory CRM capture. The 2023 revision
   (CRM-known base, "get each company to the next bridge") is close to directly reusable for the **US
   conversion thread** (US-4) — the exact same job: known dealers, direct approach, move them a stage.

## Neil's caveat on folding it in (2026-08-01)

**The archive is a shortcut, not a standard.** "The contents was never perfect either but has helped us
shortcut" — so JNY-5 imports selectively, on merit, never wholesale. Content-as-the-rep is older than this
archive for the group. **And the diagnosis of what always went missing, in Neil's words: "a filled out plan
of content per brand and the mechanical means to churn through it, record the outputs and then apply
them."** Three parts: the filled plan, the churn machinery, the record-and-apply loop. The 2023 programme
had none of the three; the current design has the machinery (S24) and the tracking (S23) — the filled
per-brand plan is the part that has still never existed, and it is the deliverable
(`../brands/c-ats/content-plan.md` is the first).

## What it warns (the honest half)

**This programme was built, agreed — "set in stone", start date 6 March, results monthly — and the estate
shows what happened next.** The 2023 ambition was 2–3 social pieces per day and a weekly mailer; the
recorded reality by 2026 is the thin estate in `../evidence/current-state.md`, and the road-model diagnosis in
`commercial-model.md` (Alan and Adrian's eight hours a week on awareness is the model that "loads the
scarce resource"). The deck's own closing slide admits the drift: "I'm not up to speed on how far we got
with the templates…" **The lesson is not that the design was wrong — it is that design without production
capacity stalls.** That is the strongest evidence yet for S24: the 2023 plan needed the production line
that now exists, and didn't have it.
