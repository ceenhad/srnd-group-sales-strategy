## 1. The authority: ADR 017 v2, `accepted`, revised 2026-08-13

**`status: accepted`, `version: 2`, `supersedes: [1, 14, 19, 73]`, `superseded_by: null`.** *This is the live
statement of what Cinema Tools is and what is sold.* **§10 answers the question this repo was asking:**

> ***"Four revenue lines carry the business — training, design services, the partner channel, product.***
> *Prices, tiers, payout percentages and market evidence live in `docs/product/`, not here."*

**So services are not an adjunct to product. Two of the four lines are services, a third is the channel that
delivers them, and product is one line of four.** *Which is what the Cinema Partners ledger already showed
(`2026-08-19-cinema-partners-accounts.md`) and what Neil said in one sentence.*

**Two more clauses bind work in this repo directly:**

- **§11 — accreditation is sought from an independent, nationally regulated awarding body. *"CEDIA accreditation,
  CEU provision and partnership are out of scope."*** *This repo's competitor and education work has repeatedly
  reasoned about CEDIA positioning; **CEDIA accreditation is settled and closed**, and nothing here should reopen it.*
- **§12 — *"demand is measured before the curriculum or the partner programme is built"* — calculator traffic first,
  then one module published to see who buys.** **This is a sequencing gate over Leg 4 and over
  `../group-strategy/partner-programme.md`.** *It does not forbid drafting; it forbids building the programme ahead
  of evidence — which is `../CLAUDE.md`'s **build it, then say it** with a measurement attached.*

## 3. What Cinema Partners is now, and why it must not be confused with our partner programme

**Then: a trading identity** — Neil, `Q80`: *"a construct to cover all of these services."*
**Now: a certified-fitter channel sold by subscription**, fed by a regulated qualification.

**These are two different instruments and this repo owns one of them:**

| | `../group-strategy/partner-programme.md` | **Cinema Partners** (platform) |
|---|---|---|
| Who | **Dealers who buy product** — registration, trade pricing, appreciation | **Fitters who deliver rooms**, by subscription |
| What it grants | Access, recognition, manufacturer-only capabilities | Leads, design, admin, credibility — a business-in-a-box |
| Its problem | The relationship runs one way and nothing goes back | Building it before demand is proven |
| Gate | This repo's own *build it, then say it* | ADR 017 §12, plus Phases 1 and 3 |

**Nothing here should be renamed or merged.** *The confusable pair is *"partner programme"* and *"Cinema Partners"*,
and the distinction is **who is being partnered**: the buyer of product, or the licensed deliverer of rooms.*

## 4. The accreditation route is researched, and the applicant it names is a brand in this repo

*`docs/_active-plans/2026-08-10-accreditation-and-cpd-routes.md`, Phase 4 of the plan above. **This answers the one
open confirmation on `../brands/c-ats/training.md` `T2`** — the CPD route, which I had recorded as unchecked.*

**The distinction the plan makes, and it is the useful part:**

| | CPD | Regulated qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Audience | Architects, specifiers, M&E — the demand side | Fitters, integrators — the supply side |
| What it does | Puts us in front of people who **commission** cinemas | Certifies people who **build** them |
| Cost | Low hundreds to low thousands | Tens of thousands + a person-year |
| Feeds | Design services | Cinema Partners |

***"Own the demand, license the delivery"*** — *and the plan is explicit that CPD is **not** a cheap substitute for
accreditation: they point at different audiences and both are wanted.*

**Four facts that change `T2` from a proposal into a candidate seminar inside an existing plan:**

1. **The obligation is real and non-discretionary.** *RIBA Chartered Members must complete **35 hours of CPD a year,
   20 of them from ten mandatory Core Curriculum topics.***
2. **The route with reach is the RIBA CPD Providers Network** *(500+ members, run by NBS/Hubexo on RIBA's behalf,
   content assessed against the Core Curriculum, distributed via NBS Source and the RIBA CPD Showcase). **Fees are
   not published.** Commercial CPD accreditors — £150–£1,600 a course — **issue a badge and carry no specifier
   distribution**, and the plan says plainly not to confuse the two.*
3. **The named applicant is a product brand, and it names ours.** *The network is manufacturer-oriented, so the
   natural applicant is **"an SRND product brand (C-ATS is a real manufacturer with real test data), not Cinema
   Tools"** — which also keeps the attribution rule clean.* **That puts the CPD route inside C-ATS's own scope.**
4. **The Quality Code decides the content.** *Material must be **factual and standards-compliant**, refreshed every
   two years, and RIBA may withdraw anything that brings it into disrepute — ***"a product pitch would not"*** meet
   it. **The `T2` draft was written as a sequence argument with no product claim in it, which is the form this
   requires**; that was luck rather than design, and it is now a constraint rather than a preference.*

**And a CPD seminar is a re-cut, not new writing.** *The plan checked all 26 Part-2 chapters against the Core
Curriculum and found **every one is Core Curriculum material with none left over** — so the seminar is existing
material re-aimed. **`PR-20` a fourth time**, and the honest reading of `T2`: it is a **narrower slice** of the
plan's own pitch — *"the room your client wants will fail on compartmentation, escape, background noise and access
unless it is designed in at Stage 2"* — **not an independent initiative.**
