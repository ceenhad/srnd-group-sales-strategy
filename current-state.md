*Why this file exists: the repo describes the strategy and the plan but never recorded **the present state it
starts from**, which is why the same facts kept arriving as surprises. This is the baseline. It is deliberately
factual and dull — no argument, no recommendations. Everything that follows from it belongs in `NEXT.md`.*

**[?]** are not known and should not be guessed at; items marked **[assumed]** are inferences to confirm or
