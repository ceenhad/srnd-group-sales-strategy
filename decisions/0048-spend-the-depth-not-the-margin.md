# ADR 0048 — Spend the depth, not the margin, and keep no ledgers

- **Status:** **Proposed** — 2026-08-17. **Not accepted.** Only Neil moves this to `accepted` (ADR 0001 §3).
- **Source:** `proposals.md` via ADR 0016, split to one decision per ADR.

## Decision

**Discount is the wrong currency for appreciation.** A percentage can be matched by anyone, and it reframes a relationship as a transaction. **No ledgers** — anything requiring a running balance is excluded by design; every element is a discrete act.

## Provenance

**Was `P1`**, then part of ADR 0016 before the split.
