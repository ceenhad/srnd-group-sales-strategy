# ADR 0017 — Who owns what between adjacent brands, and the register they all share

- **Status:** **Proposed** — 2026-08-16. **Not accepted.** Only Neil moves this to `accepted` (ADR 0001 §3).
- **Source:** `proposals.md`, consolidated. **This is a sort, not a rewrite** — the decisions below are as they
  were recorded, with their original evidence. *Nothing has been added; where the original was thin it stays
  thin.*

## Context

Adjacent brands drift into each other's territory, and a group that contradicts itself loses the
advantage coherence was meant to create.

## Decision

**1. Acoustic treatment design belongs to C-ATS.** Others cross-sell it; none duplicates it.

**2. No brand claims to be the most complex or critical part of the project.** Each is demanding in its own
terms; a superlative contest between our own brands reads as noise.

**3. DT is solutions for the architectural integration of technology** — deliberately not the projection
company.

**4. Control is embedded in DT products, never a line item.**

**5. The Scandinavian register applies group-wide.** **No superlative without a measurement behind it.**

**6. Never insinuate the dealer can't.** The design service is their choice, never a rescue.

**7. Light Walls is deferred and worked separately.**

**8. Tools are never built around our own products**, and the report is the deliverable rather than tool
access.

*Mechanics may differ per brand: C-ATS sells design as an accountable deliverable; Fabric Walls is self-serve
and should have configurators. **Do not propagate one brand's tooling rule to another.***

### Open against this

**`B7` — Light Walls deferred.** Reviewed 2026-08-15 in the LWCP repo: architecture and commercial strategy are
settled and freedom-to-operate is cleared, so *"nothing to plan here"* is no longer true even though the
deferral itself may stand. See `NEXT.md`.
