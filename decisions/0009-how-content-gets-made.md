# ADR 0009 — Judgement stays with the owner, labour moves to Claude, and there is no cadence ceremony

- **Status:** **Superseded** — 2026-08-17, split into ADRs 0027–0030, one decision each, on Neil's instruction. *A bundle cannot be accepted or rejected cleanly.*
- **Source:** `proposals.md`, consolidated. **This is a sort, not a rewrite** — the decisions below are as they
  were recorded, with their original evidence. *Nothing has been added; where the original was thin it stays
  thin.*

## Context

The constraint is planning, not performing. Being on camera is not the difficulty; arriving at the list
is — so planning splits, and only the labour moves.

## Decision

**1. An owner's primary sales output becomes authoring on camera**, scheduled like a customer meeting.

**2. Planning splits into judgement and labour; only the labour moves.** **The owner never faces a blank page**
— the editorial output is a ranked proposal to correct, not a schedule handed down.

**3. The operating loop:** archive to ranked question list to batch recording to edited and published.

**4. Content is produced at volume on a Claude-assisted line.** The substance is human and never scaled: the
records, the measured data, the archive, the settled positions. From each substance atom Claude drafts the
derivative set — canonical article, hook variants, recording prompts, datasheet copy, email items — an owner
truth-checks in minutes, and distribution is batched, source-tagged and efficient.

**5. No cadence ceremony: no set floor rate, no formal role confirmations.** **Volume of pieces, never volume
of assertions**; nothing needs to be fancy — no fresh ten-minute video every day.

### Recorded reversal

**2026-07-31 — `S20` partially reversed (Neil).** The ceremony half — **a formally set floor rate** and **a
formal confirmation of editorial ownership** — **is struck: neither was a decision anybody needed.** Production
volume comes from the Claude-assisted line instead. **What survives of `S20`: the roles are fitted in, and
nothing comes off anyone's plate** — so the publishing rate is a floor set to what the work allows rather than
a target requiring reallocation.
