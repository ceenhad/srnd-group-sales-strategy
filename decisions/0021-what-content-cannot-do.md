# ADR 0021 — Content cannot qualify, discover, close or gather intelligence

- **Status:** **Proposed** — 2026-08-17. **Not accepted.** Only Neil moves this to `accepted` (ADR 0001 §3).
- **Source:** `proposals.md` via ADR 0007, split to one decision per ADR.

## Decision

**Content cannot qualify, discover, close or gather intelligence.** The substitutes: **the tools qualify**, **the spec conversation discovers**, **Zoom and the store close**. **Market intelligence has no substitute — an open hole**, recorded rather than argued away.

## Provenance

**Was `S3`**, then part of ADR 0007 before the split.
