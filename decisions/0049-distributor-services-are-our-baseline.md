# ADR 0049 — Distributor services are our baseline, not our differentiator

- **Status:** **Proposed** — 2026-08-17. **Not accepted.** Only Neil moves this to `accepted` (ADR 0001 §3).
- **Source:** `proposals.md` via ADR 0016, split to one decision per ADR.

## Decision

**We have been a distributor for twenty years.** Credit, logistics, reps, demo rooms and training are the day job, not a capability to acquire. *A correction of an earlier misjudgement that read the parity as a threat.*

## Provenance

**Was `P2`**, then part of ADR 0016 before the split.
