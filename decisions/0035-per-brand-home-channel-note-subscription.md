# ADR 0035 — Per brand: the home, the channel, the note, the subscription

- **Status:** **Proposed** — 2026-08-17. **Not accepted.** Only Neil moves this to `accepted` (ADR 0001 §3).
- **Source:** `proposals.md` via ADR 0011, split to one decision per ADR.

## Decision

**Answers live canonically on each brand's own site**; **every brand gets its own YouTube channel** (the group channel carries group material only); the partner note runs **~monthly per brand**; and **subscriptions are managed per brand**, so a partner chooses what they receive. **`cinema-expert.com` is not the canonical home** — an assumption carried from the plan, struck.

## Provenance

**Was `S29`**, then part of ADR 0011 before the split.
