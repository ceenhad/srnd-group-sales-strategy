# ADR 0012 — The system is specified in roles; who fills them is a separate decision

- **Status:** **Proposed** — 2026-08-16. **Not accepted.** Only Neil moves this to `accepted` (ADR 0001 §3).
- **Source:** `proposals.md`, consolidated. **This is a sort, not a rewrite** — the decisions below are as they
  were recorded, with their original evidence. *Nothing has been added; where the original was thin it stays
  thin.*

## Context

A system that assigns work to named people cannot be read by anyone else, and the names change.

## Decision

**Roles, never names.** The system is specified in roles — **the owner** (judgement, truth-check, what may
be published), **the authority** (the technical face and voice), **the relationship** (the trade-facing human),
**the producer** (editorial and production, off camera). **No document assigns work to a named person**; who
fills each role — and one person may hold several — is a separate decision, made later.

*Provenance keeps names: who decided something is history, not an assignment.*
