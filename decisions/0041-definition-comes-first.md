# ADR 0041 — Definition comes before pitch, and it is missing in the middle

- **Status:** **Proposed** — 2026-08-17. **Not accepted.** Only Neil moves this to `accepted` (ADR 0001 §3).
- **Source:** `proposals.md` via ADR 0014, split to one decision per ADR.

## Decision

**You cannot state what problem a product solves until you have stated what it is and what it does** — and definition is not SKU, name and weight either. **Engine holds the object, marketing holds the pitch, and nothing holds the canonical account of what the thing is.**

**Was `S16b`**, then part of ADR 0014 before the split.
