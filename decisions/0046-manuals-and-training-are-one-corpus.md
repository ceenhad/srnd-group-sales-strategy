# ADR 0046 — Manuals and training are one corpus, differently packaged

- **Status:** **Proposed** — 2026-08-17. **Not accepted.** Only Neil moves this to `accepted` (ADR 0001 §3).
- **Source:** `proposals.md` via ADR 0015, split to one decision per ADR.

## Decision

**The manual is the reference version, training the structured version.** So manuals come first and become the raw material for the training programme rather than competing with it.

## Provenance

**Was `S12`**, then part of ADR 0015 before the split.
