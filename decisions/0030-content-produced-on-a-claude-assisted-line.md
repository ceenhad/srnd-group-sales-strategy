# ADR 0030 — Content is produced at volume on a Claude-assisted line, and there is no cadence ceremony

- **Status:** **Proposed** — 2026-08-17. **Not accepted.** Only Neil moves this to `accepted` (ADR 0001 §3).
- **Source:** `proposals.md` via ADR 0009, split to one decision per ADR.

## Decision

**Content is produced at volume on a Claude-assisted line.** The substance is human and never scaled. From each substance atom Claude drafts the derivative set — canonical article, hook variants, recording prompts, datasheet copy, email items — an owner truth-checks in minutes, and distribution is batched and source-tagged.

**No cadence ceremony: no set floor rate, no formal role confirmations.** *This struck the ceremony half of `S20` on 2026-07-31; what survives is that roles are fitted in and nothing comes off anyone's plate.*

**Was `S24`, and the `S20` reversal**, then part of ADR 0009 before the split.
