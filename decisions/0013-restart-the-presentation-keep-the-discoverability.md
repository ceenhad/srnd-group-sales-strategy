# ADR 0013 — Websites and social restart; URLs, indexed pages and channel history are kept

- **Status:** **Superseded** — 2026-08-17, split into ADRs 0037–0038, one decision each, on Neil's instruction. *A bundle cannot be accepted or rejected cleanly.*
- **Source:** `proposals.md`, consolidated. **This is a sort, not a rewrite** — the decisions below are as they
  were recorded, with their original evidence. *Nothing has been added; where the original was thin it stays
  thin.*

## Context

The sites and accounts were built ad hoc before any of this was settled, so they carry positions since
rejected. Amending page by page costs more than rebuilding to a structure already written down.

## Decision

**1. Websites and social restart; URLs, indexed pages and channel history are kept.** **The rebuild must not
gate the content cadence.**

**2. Site structure per brand and product: marketing on-ramp, then technical depth, then easy contact.** The
on-ramp states the problem solved, the opportunity opened, the time or labour saved; the depth removes doubt;
the contact route is explicit. **This is the information architecture for the rebuild.**
