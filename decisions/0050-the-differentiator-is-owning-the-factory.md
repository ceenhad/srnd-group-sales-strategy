# ADR 0050 — The differentiator is owning the brands, the factory and what the products do

- **Status:** **Proposed** — 2026-08-17. **Not accepted.** Only Neil moves this to `accepted` (ADR 0001 §3).
- **Source:** `proposals.md` via ADR 0016, split to one decision per ADR.

## Decision

**Changing the product is the thing no distributor can do.** A distributor asking a brand for something can only pass the request upstream and wait for an answer that is usually no.

## Provenance

**Was `P3`**, then part of ADR 0016 before the split.
