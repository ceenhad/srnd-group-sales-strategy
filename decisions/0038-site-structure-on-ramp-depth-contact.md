# ADR 0038 — Site structure per brand and product: on-ramp, then depth, then easy contact

- **Status:** **Proposed** — 2026-08-17. **Not accepted.** Only Neil moves this to `accepted` (ADR 0001 §3).
- **Source:** `proposals.md` via ADR 0013, split to one decision per ADR.

## Decision

**Marketing on-ramp → technical depth → easy contact.** The on-ramp states the problem solved, the opportunity opened, the time or labour saved; the depth removes doubt; the contact route is explicit.

## Provenance

**Was `S15`**, then part of ADR 0013 before the split.
