# ADR 0045 — The documentation debt is concentrated on DT, and is documented by mechanism not SKU

- **Status:** **Proposed** — 2026-08-17. **Not accepted.** Only Neil moves this to `accepted` (ADR 0001 §3).
- **Source:** `proposals.md` via ADR 0015, split to one decision per ADR.

## Decision

**C-ATS has the data — three SKUs, documented — and the debt sits mostly with DT**, then Fabric Walls and Pro-Fi. **Set the standard on C-ATS, then apply it to DT by mechanism rather than by SKU**, since the range is variant-heavy and fifty-odd products collapse into far fewer manuals.

## Provenance

**Was `S11a`, `S18`**, then part of ADR 0015 before the split.
