# ADR 0023 — The unit of work is one question, answered well, published where it can be found

- **Status:** **Proposed** — 2026-08-17. **Not accepted.** Only Neil moves this to `accepted` (ADR 0001 §3).
- **Source:** `proposals.md` via ADR 0008, split to one decision per ADR.

## Decision

**The unit of work is one question, answered well, published where it can be found** — not a campaign and not a manual. **"How do you do X" is the atom**: support, sales enablement and marketing simultaneously.

## Provenance

**Was `S14`**, then part of ADR 0008 before the split.
