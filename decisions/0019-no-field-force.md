# ADR 0019 — There is no field force and we have stopped looking for one

- **Status:** **Proposed** — 2026-08-17. **Not accepted.** Only Neil moves this to `accepted` (ADR 0001 §3).
- **Source:** `proposals.md` via ADR 0007, split to one decision per ADR.

## Decision

**There is no field force and we have stopped looking for one.**

*The diagnosis is the owner-driven model normal in this trade: a body on the road generating questions that all route back to the owner, so headcount loads the scarce resource rather than relieving it. Geography compounds it — Glasgow, the Yorkshire factory, sales leadership in Marbella.*

**Was `S1`**, then part of ADR 0007 before the split.
