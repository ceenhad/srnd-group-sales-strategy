# ADR 0025 — The corpus is the asset, not the channel

- **Status:** **Proposed** — 2026-08-17. **Not accepted.** Only Neil moves this to `accepted` (ADR 0001 §3).
- **Source:** `proposals.md` via ADR 0008, split to one decision per ADR.

## Decision

**The corpus is the asset, not the channel.** Publish canonically to owned properties, syndicate to platforms.

*The locked YouTube account demonstrated platform and personnel risk in one incident.*

## Provenance

**Was `S7`**, then part of ADR 0008 before the split.
