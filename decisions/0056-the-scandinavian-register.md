# ADR 0056 — The Scandinavian register applies group-wide: no superlative without a measurement

- **Status:** **Proposed** — 2026-08-17. **Not accepted.** Only Neil moves this to `accepted` (ADR 0001 §3).
- **Source:** `proposals.md` via ADR 0017, split to one decision per ADR.

## Decision

**Restraint over superlatives.** Substance carried in plain language and owned proper nouns, not genericised adjectives. **No superlative without a measurement behind it.**

## Provenance

**Was `B5`**, then part of ADR 0017 before the split.
