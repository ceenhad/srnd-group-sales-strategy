# ADR 0059 — Tools are never built around our own products; the report is the deliverable

- **Status:** **Proposed** — 2026-08-17. **Not accepted.** Only Neil moves this to `accepted` (ADR 0001 §3).
- **Source:** `proposals.md` via ADR 0017, split to one decision per ADR.

## Decision

**No specification-share target and no quiet weighting of the catalogue.** The tool's job is to be the best help available for whatever room is in front of the dealer. **What is sold is the report, not tool access.**

## Provenance

**Was `B8`**, then part of ADR 0017 before the split.
