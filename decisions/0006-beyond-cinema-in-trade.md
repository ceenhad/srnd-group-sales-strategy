# ADR 0006 — Beyond cinema in trade; B2C stays cinema, deliberately

- **Status:** **Proposed** — 2026-08-16. **Not accepted.** Only Neil moves this to `accepted` (ADR 0001 §3).
- **Source:** `proposals.md`, consolidated. **This is a sort, not a rewrite** — the decisions below are as they
  were recorded, with their original evidence. *Nothing has been added; where the original was thin it stays
  thin.*

## Context

High-end cinema built the reputation, the engineering and most of the revenue. None of the underlying
competences are cinema-specific — only the history is.

## Decision

**Beyond cinema in trade; B2C stays cinema.**

*In trade this is the removal of a self-imposed fence rather than a pivot — cinema stays the heartland and
should lead where it is strongest. In B2C, broadening beyond DIY home cinema would mean a vast, competitive,
low-margin market where the group holds no consumer authority, and where the communities it can reach are
cinema communities.*
