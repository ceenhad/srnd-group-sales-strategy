# ADR 0052 — Acoustic treatment design belongs to C-ATS

- **Status:** **Proposed** — 2026-08-17. **Not accepted.** Only Neil moves this to `accepted` (ADR 0001 §3).
- **Source:** `proposals.md` via ADR 0017, split to one decision per ADR.

## Decision

**Others cross-sell it; none duplicates it.** Fabric Walls carries acoustic behaviour physically, DT provides structure and decoupling, Pro-Fi handles electronic calibration and voicing.
