## Read this part first: the credit is not spendable yet

**The destination has to be live and stocked before a single video is briefed.**

The credit has sat unused precisely because there was nowhere to point it. The Cinema Store DIY range is the answer —
*the former Enthusiast screens, the small hush box, and Fabric Walls' plastic track and tools, all Cinema Store
exclusive* (`commercial-model.md`) — and the record still calls that range **proposed**.

*Pointing video at a store that cannot fulfil is `../CLAUDE.md`'s **build it, then say it** in its most expensive form:
this failure mode has already been named three times independently in this repo, and the fourth instance was found on
the C-ATS site this morning — an article promising a Room Selector that does not exist.*

**So: confirm the range is live, priced and in stock. Then brief. Not before.**

## And the second condition, because otherwise we will not know what £10k bought

**The destination needs a tracked source, or this is unmeasurable.** Engine holds `leads.lead_source_id` and
`customers.source_brand_website`; a campaign with no distinct landing route lands in the same undifferentiated pool as
everything else.

*One tracked landing page or source parameter, agreed before publication. It costs nothing at brief stage and cannot be
retrofitted afterwards.* **`ENG-27`'s sibling problem, and the same fix.**

## The audience, and why this outlet is right

**AVForums' home-cinema community is the DIY builder** — the person specifying and installing their own room, on their
own money, over months. `../CLAUDE.md` fixes the consumer proposition as **DIY home cinema, deliberately**, and this is
that audience almost exactly. It is also the audience's own word for itself: AVForums' own sections are *Members' DIY
Home Cinema* (`commercial-model.md`).

**The relationship is long-standing and worth stating in negotiation, not in content.** SRND built Picture House 4, and
AVForums published an 18-part build diary on it — but that was around ten years ago, before the group existed, and
carries no group branding. *It is evidence of a relationship. It is not an asset, and no video should reference it.*

---

## What the content is

**The format is already proven with this audience by this outlet: a build.** An 18-part build diary is what they made
last time and what that community watches.

**Proposal: a build series using the Cinema Store range, presented by AVForums, briefed and not scripted.**

*Why briefed and not scripted: editorial independence is the value of the placement. A scripted video from a
manufacturer is an advert with a forum logo on it, and this audience is unusually good at spotting one. A presenter who
found something awkward and said so is worth more than three that did not.*

**What we want a viewer to come away with, in priority order:**

1. **That a room like this is buildable by them.** The whole proposition is DIY. If the series makes it look like
   something only a professional does, it has sold the wrong thing.
2. **That the components are made rather than badged.** The group designs and manufactures in North Yorkshire. That is
   true, it is unusual in this market, and it is stated as fact rather than as a boast.
3. **Where to get it.** One destination, one route, tracked.

**What we do not want:** a specification walkthrough, a feature list, or anything that reads as a product launch.

## Constraints

| Constraint | Why |
|---|---|
| **No trade brand names.** Not Display Technologies, Fabric Walls, Light Walls, C-ATS, Pro-Fi or SRND Distribution | The separation is what makes the placement safe. Products appear as Cinema Store products (`channels.md`) |
| **No C-ATS at all** | Treatment and the design service are channel business, not consumer (`commercial-model.md`) |
| **No prices spoken in the video** | Not a rule, a practicality — prices date and the video does not. The store carries them, which is where an end user is allowed to see them (`Q42`) |
| **No unmeasured claims** | House rule, and it applies hardest where nobody is checking. No superlatives without a measurement behind them |
| **No competitor named** | `../CLAUDE.md`. Criticise a category if it is true; name nobody |
| **Nothing implying trade terms or partnership** | This is a consumer channel and must not read as a trade recruitment route |

## What we supply, and what we ask for

**Ours:** product, access, a technical contact who can answer a presenter's awkward question on camera, and the
tracked destination.

**Theirs:** the edit, the running order, and the opinions. *We approve nothing except factual accuracy and the
constraints above — which is the whole point of briefing rather than scripting, and it needs saying to them in those
words so the boundary is clear from the start.*
