who

Four roles, never names

The whole system is specified in roles — one person may hold several, and who fills each is a separate decision made later. Provenance keeps names; assignments do not.

the owner

Judgement, truth-check, and what may be published. Approves a ranked list, confirms what is true today, records answers in batches — never authors the list.

Supplies H1–H3 · G2 G3 · K1–K3 · runs the review route minutes per approval; one recording session per batch
the authority

The technical face and voice. The technical answer, the impossible project, and later the only route to the specifier — a CPD seminar is a person.

Supplies N1–N4 · D1–D10 · X2 X3 X4 · the voice behind every N3 answer the scarce input; spend it on substance, never on production
the relationship

The trade-facing human. The welcome, the walkthrough, the invitation, the follow-up — anything that ends in a conversation.

Supplies O1 · R3 · X1 X5 X6 · W2 · runs the deal route light, and already part of the sales job
the producer

Editorial and production, off camera. Drafts and ranks the list, briefs, produces, publishes, keeps it current. Knowing what needs discussing is the scarcer skill — which is why this role stays behind the camera.

Owns the record itself · turns N3 into the corpus real and ongoing — the one genuine reallocation

A fifth commitment sits alongside these in the plan — the trade view: which questions dealers actually ask, and the follow-up nobody currently owns. It is not a casting role, but it is the input R3 depends on.

the estate

Three tiers of ignorance, not one gap

Measured as fuel for the production line rather than as inventory — the records are step one of that line, the substance it multiplies from.

the finding

Three ways of looking, one answer

the domain map said

Defending had one field. Twelve domains mapped against the form, and the whole job of holding a product against its rivals was a single line called comparison.

the generation gates said

Comparison content cannot be produced. Not thin, not stale — there is no source in the record from which to draft it at all.

the task map said

Nobody performs the work that would feed it. Inventorying a rep's and a marketing executive's actual week turns up five tasks with no performer.

five tasks · one job · nobody is looking outward
competitive responsemarket intelligencecompetitor tracking product feedbackwhy deals are lost

Recorded as evidence that an existing hole is wider than it was logged as — not as a reopening of the decision. Whether it becomes a role, a standing habit, or an accepted hole recorded at its true size is SYS-6, and it belongs to the owners.

Two more confirmations from a second direction: N7 — material a dealer hands their own client — is an ordinary marketing task with no performer and an empty field, in a group whose stated advantage is depth spent on the dealer's behalf. And product launch has no owner at all: the Screen Wall, demonstrable since ISE 2023 with no page and no datasheet, is an unassigned task rather than a content failure.

how it gets filled

Six routes — a route is a task whose output is caught

Fifty-eight fields is not twelve projects and not one documentation push. A running route fills its domains forever; a field filled by a push starts decaying the day it is written. Five of the six ride on events that already happen.

The build A product is made or changed D1–D10 · L1 L2 per change
The test A test is commissioned; a report lands D9 · G1 · G4 rare
The design desk A job is designed, or spec help given — our only discovery channel N1 N2 N5 · D4 D5 daily
The support line A question is answered, pre-sale or on site R3 R4 N3 · L3 L4 daily
The deal A quote goes out — and above all, a deal is lost X1–X6 · M1–M5 · O1 caught nowhere today
The owners A review; a position settled H1–H3 · G2 G3 · K1–K3 the one added habit

The lost deal is the highest-value capture event in the business and nothing catches it. It is the only outside view of which competitor, on which dimension, at which stage — one line at the moment of losing, worth more than any amount of desk research on rivals.
