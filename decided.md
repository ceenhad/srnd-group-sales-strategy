# Decided

**Business and management decisions, each one traceable to the question that produced it.** *A thing is decided only
when it went through the question box and Neil answered it (`registers/questions.md`). This file is where those
decisions are stored in a fixed shape so they can be read, cited and acted on — by people and by SRND OS.*

> **All thirteen approved by Neil, at the gate, reviewed as a set** — `https://claude.ai/code/artifact/80b5a86a-a58d-460f-8f38-183f706dc742`.
> *`D-01` to `D-13` are live. Anything added later enters the same way: put to him, reviewed, approved.*

**Repo management never goes in here.** *Filing conventions, wording choices and corrections of this repo's own
reasoning are not decisions the business acts on. That distinction is the whole reason this file was emptied once
before.*

## The record shape

*Every entry carries the same six fields, in the same order.*

`ID` — permanent, never recycled · `Decision` — one line, the position itself · `Kind` — pricing · policy ·
publication · commitment · spend · naming · product · content · `Scope` — group, or a brand, or platform ·
`Question` — the `Q` ids in `registers/questions.md` that produced it · `Words` — Neil's own, verbatim ·
`Acts on` — what is done differently because of it.

## D-01 · The acoustic treatment design service is a single fee per room, and that fee is about £1,500

- **Kind** pricing · **Scope** c-ats · **Question** `Q89`, `Q94` · **Decided** 2026-08-21
- **Words** *"Single fee per room."* · *"Around £1,500."*
- **Acts on** A price exists where there was none. Roughly 38 % below the £2,400 median across 41 historic design
  engagements, and deliberately not proportional to the time Cinema Tools saved — that gain is ours, not the buyer's.
  Unblocks quoting the design step, and the `T3` training subject.

## D-02 · Product fact publishes; anything that advances the sale sits behind a login

- **Kind** policy · **Scope** group · **Question** `Q90` · **Decided** 2026-08-21
- **Words** *"anything that sniffs of getting on the sales ladder like reccomended layouts etc should be gated by
  login."*
- **Acts on** A gating test replaces case-by-case judgement. Exact 3D CAD never leaves — it has been copied off other
  products already. 2D product drawings are free. Recommended layouts, worked examples, the design hierarchy and the
  budget formula gate, and become the registration incentive that `G3` currently lacks.

## D-03 · Declare the current test data the limit; our own fire certificates are the right answer, held back on cost

- **Kind** commitment · **Scope** c-ats · **Question** `Q86` · **Decided** 2026-08-21
- **Words** *"the correct answer is to get our own fire test certificates but the cost just hasnt justified it."*

## D-04 · What a specifier receives is the material certificate, not a panel test

- **Kind** policy · **Scope** c-ats · **Question** `Q84` · **Decided** 2026-08-21
- **Words** *"the material certificate is what gets supplied."* · *"and it was Llyods themsevles that requested that
  material."*
- **Acts on** The claim boundary is explicit: the material holds the certificate, the panel does not, and nothing may
  blur the two. Lloyd's Register specified the marine material themselves, which gives a publishable line that names
  nothing — *"the material specified by Lloyd's Register."*

## D-05 · The published marine coefficient sheet stands; no marine test is needed

- **Kind** publication · **Scope** c-ats · **Question** `Q83` · **Decided** 2026-08-21
- **Words** *"no. the marine panel is hand mad in 3mm hi-macs material using press molds that deliver the same shape as
  the standard ref-panel. the absorbtion characterisic is not at the heart of a ref-panel so using the same data as the
  bonded is representative. the marine panel is a hand made product 25x the cost or more of the standard injection
  moulded part and is only needed in very specific cases under demand from Lyods of london."*
- **Acts on** A published document survives a challenge, with a stated reason — identical geometry from press moulds.
  What is owed is one line stating the basis wherever those figures appear (`DOC-33`). And marine stays demoted for a
  recorded reason: hand made, 25× the cost or more, only on a Lloyd's demand.

## D-06 · Material identity never appears in public content

- **Kind** policy · **Scope** group · **Question** `Q85` · **Decided** 2026-08-21
- **Words** *"one issue is that we of course prefer not to have the brand names of the foams etc on the website. a Rev
  panel we sell for X is literally the same an enterprising delaer could be for Y directly from e-foam etc."*
- **Acts on** An existing hard don't gains the commercial reason that makes it enforce itself: naming the material tells
  a dealer where to buy it instead. And it identifies one panel in three as substitutable at material cost, which
  nothing had recorded. A compliance certificate names the material, so it is a gated project-basis disclosure, never a
  download.

## D-07 · Transcribe the material fire data onto our own datasheet

- **Kind** publication · **Scope** c-ats · **Question** `Q88` · **Decided** 2026-08-21
- **Words** *"efoam just copied it from vita badly. we will just do the same."*
- **Acts on** A C-ATS datasheet carries the classification without a supplier's name. Two rules travel with it:
  transcribe from the manufacturer's own sheet rather than a reseller's, recording source and date — the reseller has
  the density at 23.5–25.5 kg/m³ against an actual 85, which is what "badly" means; and attribute the class to the core
  material rather than to the panel.

## D-08 · The 300 mm layout tolerance is withdrawn; relative positions matter, laser accuracy does not

- **Kind** publication · **Scope** c-ats · **Question** `Q102`, `Q103` · **Decided** 2026-08-21
- **Words** *"the 300mm no longer holds due to the massive advantage of aperiodic layout."* · *"its doesnt require laser
  accuracy still but the relative positions do matter."*
- **Acts on** A claim comes off every surface it reached, and the install instruction inverts: set out from the drawing,
  shift the run rather than one panel, never substitute an arrangement. It also makes the design fee legible — if the
  pattern is what performs, the layout is worth paying for, where the old claim argued the opposite.
